Statistics count. Also for you.

The Federal Statistical Office (BFS) is the national centre of excellence for public statistics and a key player in the Swiss data ecosystem. It produces statistical information, works with the national data management for harmonised data and develops the knowledge gained from data using data science methods. The BFS thus contributes to the formation of public opinion and makes an important contribution to modern democratic governance.

Additional information

The Federal Statistical Office plays an active role in shaping national and international data and statistical policy and represents Switzerland’s interests in central national and international bodies. As Head of Section for International and National Affairs, you lead a versatile organisational unit and, together with the Head of Department for Foreign Relations and Communication (AK), shape the positioning of the FSO vis-à-vis authorities, international organisations, partner institutions and other stakeholders. They are thus making a direct contribution to the further development of Switzerland’s public data and statistics system, to the implementation of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and to the establishment of the new Foreign Relations and Communications (AK) department, with the function focusing on the FFO’s national coordination and networking.
