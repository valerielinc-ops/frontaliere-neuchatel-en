Full description

Our rehabilitation units are located at the sites of Locle, Val-de-Ruz, and offer different types of courses. Our patients benefit from a multidisciplinary and personalized care thanks to a dynamic and specialized team working on a state-of-the-art technical platform.

Nurse head of care unit at 80-100%

Your missions

  • Lead and mentor a team of about 30 employees
  • Manage and coordinate the organization of nursing care in a 24-bed unit with general rehabilitation beds
  • Ensure efficient patient care and quality of care in accordance with existing protocols and recommendations
  • Ensuring the application of principles of professional ethics and the dissemination of care values
  • Functional supervision of teams, support for new arrivals and the development of skills within its department (continuous training)
  • Optimally organize unity in collaboration with different partners

Your profile

  • Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing or equivalent title recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Authorization to practise a health profession in the canton of Neuchâtel
  • Minimum work experience 3 years in hospital
  • An experience in rehabilitation, an asset
  • Management training, community management or willingness to attend training
  • Team management experience, an asset

Your skills

  • Ability to lead, manage and unite a team
  • A sense of responsibility and natural leadership
  • Relationship skills in communication and conflict management
  • Teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Sense of priorities, ability to manage the unexpected, and its stress
  • Be proposal strength, flexible and available
  • Common software knowledge, one plus (Outlook, Carefolio, POLYPOINT, Opale)
