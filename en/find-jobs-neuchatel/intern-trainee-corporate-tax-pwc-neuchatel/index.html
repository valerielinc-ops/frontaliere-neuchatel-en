Full description

Within PwC, you will join a dynamic and collaborative team, specialized in accompaniment to local and international clients on various and stimulating tax issues. In a constantly changing environment, you will contribute to projects with high added value, while developing your technical, digital and relational skills alongside experienced professionals

  • Advise companies on complex fiscal, economic and legal aspects, providing innovative solutions, including the use of modern technology tools and artificial intelligence solutions.
  • Guide our clients on tax compliance, tax accounting, and support them in the transformation of their tax function, in order to optimize the management of their obligations and support the evolution of their processes using powerful digital tools.
  • Work on a young and dynamic team and participate in research and the development of tax advice in the form of tax agreements, memoranda, opinions or presentations for clients.
  • Collaborate effectively with different stakeholders, demonstrating strong communication skills, both in the oral and in writing, in order to convey technical topics in a clear, structured and accessible manner.
  • Take advantage of excellent career prospects: after six months, if you feel comfortable in the position, you could move to a consultant position.
  • Receive personalized support throughout your internship, while enriching your technical skills through ongoing training on current tax topics, as well as on the new technological tools offered by our Managers and Associates.
  • Currently holds a master's degree in economics, business administration or law, in Switzerland.
  • Have a strong interest in national and international taxation, as well as the willingness to continue to deepen your expertise in this area.
  • Have the ambition to become a tax expert and, as such, consider vocational training leading to the federal tax expert degree.
  • Demonstrate a natural affinity with numbers and master common IT tools such as Excel and PowerPoint, while having a strong interest in technology tools in general, digital solutions, artificial intelligence and data analysis.
  • Be comfortable with learning and using new technology tools, and have the curiosity to understand how AI and emerging technologies can support and transform tax professions.
  • Master English in oral and written language, in addition to your French mother tongue. German would be an asset.
  • Have very good communication and presentation skills, with the ability to exchange clearly, professionally and effectively with clients and colleagues.
  • Want to work as a team, help solve complex issues and provide quality support to your clients and colleagues.
  • Demonstrate a service-oriented attitude, a natural sense of initiative and a strong commitment to your work, with constant concern to provide excellent quality work.
  • Demonstrate a real capacity for learning, adaptation and collaboration, both in a team environment and in autonomy.
