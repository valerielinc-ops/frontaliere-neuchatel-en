Full description

Facing the challenges of our time Help us grow and be more impactful! The “Integrated and Wireless Systems” Business Unit , based in Neuchâtel, Switzerland is looking for a RF IC Design Engineer .

Your mission

Bridge research and industry working alongside experienced engineers, contributing to circuit design, simulation, implementation, and silicon validation while developing technical expertise. Participate in the design of ultra-low-power wireless transceivers for narrowband communications such as Bluetooth, UWB solutions for ranging and localization, and mm-wave radar systems for sensing applications.

Your responsibilities

  • Design and verify RF and analog circuit blocks for research and industrial projects, contributing to circuit specifications and integration with support from experienced designers.
  • Perform circuit simulations to assess noise, linearity, stability, and robustness across PVT corners and Monte Carlo variations.
  • Contribute to RF layout, parasitic extraction, and electromagnetic simulations, and characterize silicon in the laboratory to compare measurements with simulations.
  • Organize and document your design activities, participate in design reviews, and deliver results in line with project requirements and deadlines.
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams and external partners, participate in technical conferences and customer visits, and contribute to technical publications and new ideas.

Your profile PhD or MSc in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, or a closely related field. Know-how

  • Practical experience in analog or RF IC design through academic, research, or industrial projects. Experience with silicon implementation and measurement is an advantage.
  • Solid foundations in low-power and low-noise analog and RF circuit design in CMOS, including trade-offs involving power, noise, linearity, and silicon area.
  • Understanding of wireless transceiver architectures and design experience with one or more building blocks, such as LNAs, mixers, power amplifiers, oscillators, PLLs, or filters.
  • Familiarity with Cadence design tools and circuit verification, including RF, PVT, and Monte Carlo simulations, and awareness of layout and parasitic effects.
  • Familiarity with RF laboratory equipment and measurement techniques. Knowledge of electromagnetic simulation, measurement automation, or data analysis is an advantage.

Interpersonal skills

  • Technical curiosity, a willingness to learn, and an analytical, hands-on approach to solving problems.
  • A collaborative mindset, with the ability to work in a multidisciplinary and multicultural team and seek feedback when needed.
  • A proactive and organized approach, taking ownership of assigned tasks and communicating progress and technical challenges clearly.
  • Good verbal and written communication skills in English
  • French and German valued.
