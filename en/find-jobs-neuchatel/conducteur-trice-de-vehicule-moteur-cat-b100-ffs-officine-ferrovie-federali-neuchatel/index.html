Cat. B100 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cat. B100 Neuchâtel, 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You will find a varied work in a motivated team on interesting sites rather in the Neuchâtel region. We are proud to offer attractive working conditions, even in the case of irregular working hours. You consider irregular schedules and teamwork as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. At home, night and weekend work entitles us to extra pay. You also benefit from time increases. This means you have more free time than employees who do not work at night or on weekends. To find out what other benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, free AG 2nd class or reduced-cost trips abroad (also for the family), visit our website under "Social Benefits". What you can move.

  • As a motor vehicle driver, you safely drive contact line construction trains and help on the contact line while complying with applicable regulations. If you are ready to take the B100 locomotive pilot training, we offer you a second paid training.
  • A high degree of security within the team is to be guaranteed while respecting the working processes.
  • You are responsible for the preparation and monitoring of trains and you perform services related to our businesses according to production needs.
  • The field of activity also extends to the entry and transmission of data relating to the operation of freight trains using the computer tools at your disposal.

What you bring.

  • You have great motivation, you are ready to take responsibility, you are passionately involved in your tasks and you have high quality requirements.
  • Ready to work outside office hours, you also ensure the picket service.
  • Your sense of communication allows you to convey complex information in a clear and understandable way and to meet the needs of different stakeholders.
  • Technical professional (CFC) qualified for the operation of B100 locomotives and heavy rail machinery (Combi-Krani, TM 234AK), specialized in the maintenance of contact lines or ready for training as a pilot of B100 locomotives.
  • You communicate with ease in French (C1) and you have knowledge in German.
  • Perfect colour vision (no prot anomaly or deutera) and no entry is included in your criminal record or prosecution registry.
