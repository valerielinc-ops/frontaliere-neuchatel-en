Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The department of adult, research, for its legal field at the Department of Medicine and Penitentiary Psychiatry (SMPP) from Bellevue to Gorgier, one·e:

Nurse at 80%

As part of your function you will be required to

  • carry out prescribed or proper nursing care to maintain or restore the health of persons in detention
  • ensure transition follow-up for persons who leave detention to the open environment (transition case management model)
  • collaborate with different interdisciplinarity stakeholders
  • informing detained persons of their health rights, duties and responsibilities
  • implementing and monitoring the application of the concordatary rules and the internal rules of the institutions concerning the functioning of the medical sector

The competencies required for this position are

  • holding a HES/ES diploma or a qualification deemed equivalent and recognised by the Swiss Red Cross
  • have a blank criminal record
  • driver's licence and private vehicle essential for travelling on both sites (EDPR and EEPB)
  • Demonstrate commitment to the responsibilities assigned to the MPMS security mission
  • know the Swiss health system and the Neuchâtel health network
  • be mobile and have an excellent capacity for personal organization and adaptation to changes
  • working independently and dealing with crisis situations
