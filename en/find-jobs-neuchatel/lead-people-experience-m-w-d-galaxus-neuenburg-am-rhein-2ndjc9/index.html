Full description

As Lead People Experience, you lead a growing team of four and are jointly responsible for the management of our logistics sites in Neuchâtel and Krefeld. Mansioni

  • You bring at least two to three years of leadership experience and have ideally already led a team of seven to ten employees
  • HR is not a black box for you. You have in-depth expertise and are very familiar with German employment law
  • You also make informed decisions in a dynamic environment, set clear priorities and can represent them convincingly.
  • Working with different stakeholders and leaders at all levels is easy for you and gives you pleasure
