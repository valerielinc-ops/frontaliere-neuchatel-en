Full description

For our two new logistics centers in Neuchâtel am Rhein in Germany, we are looking for an independent person as an HR specialist in our People & Culture department. Our ambitious growth plans for the Neuchâtel am Rhein site offer you the opportunity to grow and actively shape the future of HR at the logistics centre.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Neuchâtel am Rhein, Hans-Buck-Straße 3, 7939 Neuchâtel am Rhein

Mansioni

  • From the drafting of the employment contract to the preparation of the exit - you are responsible for the personnel administration from A to Z
  • You create intermediate and employment certificates and process mutations, applications for unpaid leave, side jobs, etc.
  • You advise our employees on various aspects of our employment conditions, time recording and regulations
  • As part of our quarterly goals and daily business, you will work on the continuous optimization of our processes, with a focus on digitalization, automation and simplicity.
  • The postal processing and the digital storage of personal documents in the electronic personal dossier are also part of your tasks
  • As the first contact person, you support our employees on site and take their requests competently

Requirements

  • Completed initial or further training: Personnel administration
  • You have 3-5 years of relevant work experience in a similar role and bring solid knowledge in the German personnel administration as well as in labour and social security law
  • You have an affinity for digital tools and find your way around the different softwares (Success Factors, Abacus, MyTime, Zendesk, Confluence and Jira)
  • You enjoy discovering opportunities for improvement and implementing changes that promote productivity and efficiency – while not losing sight of people
  • You think systemically and beyond your area of responsibility and let a good dose of pragmatism flow into your decisions
  • You communicate effectively in both German and English. French skills are an absolute plus
  • You are a real team player who likes to support others and at the same time does not hesitate to ask for help if necessary. Through consistent and reliable action, you build trust
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • Be resilient in changing times and tackle change positively
  • German (flowing)
  • English (flowing)
