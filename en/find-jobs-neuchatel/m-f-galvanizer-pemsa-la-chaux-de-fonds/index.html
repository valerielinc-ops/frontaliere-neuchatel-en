Your assignment

  • Manage and ensure the proper functioning of a galvanoplasty line.
  • Fill the basins and ensure compliance with quality requirements.
  • Do the necessary monitoring, analysis and corrections of treatment baths.
  • Ensure production fluidity and compliance with quality standards.
  • Ensure monitoring of production plants, including STEP equipment.
  • To actively participate in the optimization of processes and means of production.
  • Provide technical support to the production manager.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Professional diploma (CFC) in galvanoplasty or equivalent training.
  • Comprehensive experience in surface treatments or galvanoplasty.
  • Good mastery of bath analysis and correction.
  • A sense of responsibility, rigour and autonomy.
  • Excellent analytical and communication skills.
  • Team spirit and will to engage in long-term collaboration.
  • Knowledge of computer science is an advantage.
