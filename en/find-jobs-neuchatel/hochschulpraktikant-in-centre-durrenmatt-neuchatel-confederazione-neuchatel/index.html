Living memory of Switzerland for all

The Swiss National Library collects Helvetica - texts, images and sounds of Switzerland - and secures the collective memory of Switzerland with its collections. In cooperation with other institutions, it provides wide access to the country’s cultural heritage.

Built by Mario Botta, the Centre Dürrenmatt Neuchâtel (CDN), which integrates the old home of the Swiss painter and writer Friedrich Dürrenmatt, was opened in 2000. Its purpose is to collect and preserve the work of Friedrich Dürrenmatt and to make it known in dialogue with his literary work. Temporary exhibitions and events take place regularly. Every year, several CDN Cahier are published. The CDN is a bilingual institution.
