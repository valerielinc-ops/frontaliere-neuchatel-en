Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Directorate of Finance, for its medical secretariat sector, is seeking to fill the post of:

90% medical secretary

Holder of an SWC of a trade employee or an equivalent title, you have 5 years of minimum administrative experience. The medical secretary degree is an asset. You want to join a recognized institution specializing in mental health.

As part of your function you will be required to

o play an interface role with patients, families and partners by ensuring a reception professional and courteous physical and telephone o planning appointments and participating in agenda management o ensure the quality, reliability and continuity of administrative tasks related to patient management o manage the flow of documentation (mail processing, follow-up and rereading of reports, requests for files, etc.) and ensure proper maintenance of medical records o work closely with and provide effective and proactive administrative support to medical and multidisciplinary teams
