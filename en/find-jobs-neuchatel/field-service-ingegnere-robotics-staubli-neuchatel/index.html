Full description

To ensure the ongoing support of our customers in Switzerland, we are looking for a motivated person with strong technical skills to fill the position of Field Service Engineer, based in the Neuchâtel region. In this exciting role, you will work as part of a dynamic customer service team and make an important contribution to the availability and performance of our robotic solutions. The focus is on French-speaking Switzerland, with occasional assignments in other regions of the country.

Your responsibilities

  • Perform preventive maintenance and repair work on robotic systems directly at customer sites (approximately 75% travel)
  • Analysis and lasting elimination of technical faults, as well as carrying out repairs
  • Close cooperation with customers and internal departments to ensure first-rate service
  • Technical support and advice to our customers (hotline)
  • Documentation and tracking of service cases in the CRM system
  • Management and replenishment of spare parts via SAP

Candidate profile

  • Training in a technical field and/or automation
  • Several years of experience as a field service technician
  • Ideally, experience in robotics; alternatively, knowledge of precision mechanics, automation or related technical fields
  • Very good knowledge of French (C2) and good command of English (C1); knowledge of German is an asset
  • Strong analytical skills and a structured, independent way of working
  • Strong customer orientation and very good communication skills
  • Flexibility, willingness to travel and a professional demeanor even in demanding situations
  • A team spirit that enables you to contribute positively to cohesion and mutual support within the group

We offer

  • Varied work in an innovative, forward-looking environment
  • Attractive working conditions and modern equipment
  • In-depth product and technical training
  • A high level of personal responsibility and development opportunities
  • Collaboration within a professional, collegial and high-performing team

We look forward to meeting you and shaping the future of robotics in Switzerland together. Apply today!
