Full description

Reference code: JR129923

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

WHAT WILL YOUR CONTRIBUTION BE?

As a member of our R&I Electronic Systems & Solutions (E2S) team, you will be the conductor of complex and impacting projects:

Piloting Innovation Projects

Conduct the scoping, planning and monitoring of strategic R&I projects, from the exploratory study phase to technology transfer.

Define and manage objectives, deliverables, schedules and risks, using Agile and Prince2 methodologies.

Coordinate and lead technical teams, internal partners (Manufactures, Houses) and external partners, ensuring smooth and effective collaboration.

Prepare and formalize project documentation at each key stage.

Firmware & Ultra Low Power Technical Expertise

Design and deploy robust and scalable embedded software architectures capable of supporting the development of our products.

Develop proof of concept (POC) and prototypes, with an integrated system approach (hardware, software, mechanics).

Implement and validate high-performance firmware in C, C++ and Python, with recognized expertise in STM32 microcontrollers and EFR32 knowledge.

Master power optimization at a micro-amperium and nano-amperium level, finely managing standby modes, using MCU and peripherals to achieve ultra-low consumption.

Integrate and pilot complex sensors via interfaces such as SPI, I2C, BLE, NFC and bitbang techniques.

Implement rigorous development practices, including the use of GIT for version control, embedded debugging and JTAG.

Technology Watch & Innovation

To be the driving force for proposals on innovative technologies, concepts and development axes that support the Group's R&I strategy.

Ensure active technological monitoring of the latest advances in embedded, microelectronic and energy management systems.

Support for industrialization

Provide expert technical support to Manufactures and Houses during the transition to production and ensure the quality and robustness of deployed solutions.

WHAT KEYS ARE YOUR SUCCESS?

Electronics engineer, embedded systems, microtechnics or equivalent.

You justify a first significant experience (3 to 5 years) in firmware development and management of complex technical projects, with recognized expertise in ultra-low consumption.

Technical expertise

Deep mastery of languages C and Python.

Expertise in embedded software architecture and ability to design scalable solutions.

Proven knowledge of STM32 microcontrollers and familiarity with EFR32.

Essential expertise in energy optimization for ultra-low power applications (targets nA/μA).

Knowledge of communication interfaces (SPI, I2C, BLE, NFC, bitbang).

Practice of integrating sensors.

Mastering version management tools like GIT.

Experience in on-board debugging and use of JTAG.

Experience in project management; knowledge of Agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban) and Prince2, a plus.

With a high degree of autonomy, a strong sense of organization and excellent communication skills, you can unite teams, take initiatives and solve complex problems with rigour and analysis. You are proactive.ve in exploring and adopting innovative tools, including those based on Artificial Intelligence, to improve development efficiency.

Master's degree in English and French, oral and written.

The position is based mainly in Buttes, in the heart of our innovation laboratory, with a regular presence in our R&I hub in Neuchâtel to promote team exchanges and synergies. We offer a flexible and balanced working environment thanks to flexible working hours and the possibility of a telework day per week.

This is a 12-month post, with the possibility of renewal.

YOUR CANDIDATE EXPERIENCE WITH US

If your application is successful, we will invite you to an interview with the Pôle Manager and HR Business Partner at our office. In a second step, we will submit a practical case to you and come before a panel of experts.
