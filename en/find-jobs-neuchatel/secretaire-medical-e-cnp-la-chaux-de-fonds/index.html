Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims at the rehabilitation of patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Finance Directorate, for its medical secretariat sector, for the adult department, is looking for:

Medical Secretary 90% - CDI s/ref: 2026192

A holder of an SWC who is a commercial employee or has an equivalent qualification, you ideally have a medical secretary's degree (attraction) and 3 to 5 years of administrative experience. You want to join a recognized institution specializing in mental health.

As part of your function you will be required to

  • Ensure quality patient and accompanying care and reception,
  • Support phone calls,
  • Manage and optimize calendars (planning and tracking appointments),
  • Create, update and archive medical records,
  • Working closely with medical and multidisciplinary teams,
  • Participate in symposia and take the VP,
  • Process mail and various administrative tasks.

The competencies required for this position are

  • Holder of a SWC of a trade employee or an equivalent title,
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in the administrative field,
  • Common use of the Office suite and ideally, knowledge of software in force at the CNP (Opale, Polypoint, Carefolio).
  • Relationship strength and ability to cope with stress situations,
  • Organizational sense and effective management of priorities,
  • Rigor and accuracy,
  • Perfect mastery of the French language in oral and written form,
  • Autonomy and ability to work as a team,
  • Dynamism, openness and speed of execution,
  • Demonstrate restraint in the disclosure of information and ensure confidentiality.
