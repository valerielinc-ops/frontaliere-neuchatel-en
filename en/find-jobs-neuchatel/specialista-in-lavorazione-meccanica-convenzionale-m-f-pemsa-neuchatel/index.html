Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of a fixed employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of Specialist in conventional mechanical machining (M/F)

Your assignment

  • Make prototypes and small series by conventional processing.
  • Mainly perform conventional milling works.
  • Participate, according to need, to the turning and grinding operations flat.
  • Read and interpret technical drawings in order to ensure the conformity of the pieces produced.
  • Check the quality of the pieces made and ensure compliance with the tolerances required.
  • Respect production times and quality standards in force.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Diploma CFC of polymechanics or training considered equivalent.
  • Experienced in conventional processing.
  • Excellent knowledge of the methods of processing and reading technical drawings.
  • Mastery of instruments of measurement and control of common use.
  • Independent, precise and conscientious person.
  • Team spirit, sense of responsibility and ease of communication.
