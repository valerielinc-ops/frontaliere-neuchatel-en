The Bank Raiffeisen Neuchâtel and Vallées, a bank very well established at the local level with agencies on the Littoral, Val-de-Ruz and Val-de-Travers, is looking for a collaborator or a collaborator to assist the Head of Human Resources.

What are you waiting for?

Administrative human resources management

  • To ensure the administrative follow-up of employees throughout their career in the bank;
  • Manage and maintain personnel records;
  • Process correspondence for management;
  • Contribute to the preparation of internal documents, reports and communications.

Monitoring of internal projects

  • Actively participate in the implementation of cross-cutting projects;
  • Monitoring and project deadlines;
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of administrative and HR processes.

What are you bringing?

  • HR certificate or other human resources training;
  • Autonomy and sense of initiative marked;
  • Good project management capacity;
  • Writing experience.

Join an HR team that works at the heart of the bank, closest to employees. Céline Rufener Eschler Human Resources Manager Why Raiffeisen?
