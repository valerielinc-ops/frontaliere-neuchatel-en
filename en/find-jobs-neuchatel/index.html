Cost of living vs Ticino

The median gross salary in Neuchâtel is about 22% higher than in Ticino (BFS LSE 2024 regional wage-level data). The median LAMal health-insurance premium is also higher: CHF 479/month vs CHF 425/month in Ticino (13% higher, BAG/UFSP data) — a real extra cost to factor into your net income.

G permit and border zone

Neuchâtel borders Germany and France, not Italy: the G permit here is typically issued to German or French residents of the border zone. Italian residents considering a role in Neuchâtel usually apply for a B residence permit instead of cross-border-commuter status, which is reserved for residents of a border municipality in the neighbouring country.

Working in the canton of Neuchâtel as a cross-border worker

The canton of Neuchâtel is one of the main destinations for Italian cross-border workers (frontalieri) chasing salaries that exceed the Italian average. To qualify for the cross-border tax regime under the 2026 New Agreement you must reside in Italy within 20 km of the Swiss border, return home daily (with up to 25 % of days allowed in smart-working from Italy), and hold a G-type work permit.
