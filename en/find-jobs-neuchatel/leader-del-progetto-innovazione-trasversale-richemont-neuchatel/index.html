Full description

Reference code: JR136298

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

YOUR MISSIONS

Pilot strategic projects (70%): You lead strategic innovation projects across the Group, from scoping to delivery.

  • Project management: you align the needs with the teams, structure their contributions and coordinate the various projects with the Group's entities. You follow the actions until they are completed, using the existing project management and innovation tools and methods.
  • Federation teams: Thanks to your leadership and your sense of collaboration, you mobilize multidisciplinary teams from our different Houses and functions around common goals. You link the scientific experts to the Houses, and guarantee the adherence and success of the initiatives.

Support to R&I organization (30%): Helping Professionalize the Organization's Practices and Tools

  • Project management tools and methods: you are evolving existing tools, templates (templates) and the internal project management platform, collecting user needs and defining expected functionality. You also support teams in documenting and sharing their knowledge, including through the integration of AI tools.
  • Internal R&I communication: In connection with Internal Communication and R&I teams, you coordinate the production of media (activity report, Viva Engage publications, communications via Reach). You are also contributing to the organization of Innovation Day, the Group's flagship innovation event, which promotes encounters and fertilization.

WHAT MAKES THIS SINGLE POSITION

  • A visible impact: Your projects are sponsored at the management level, what you deliver counts for the group.
  • An intellectual variety: Watch, industrial optimization, digital tools, event... No week is the same.
  • A strategic posture: You do not undergo projects, you frame them, structure them and move them forward.
  • An expanded network: You work with all Houses and experts from a wide variety of fields.

YOUR PROFILE

You do not need to check all the boxes, but you recognize yourself in this description:

  • Structural Spirit: You know how to take a vague subject and make it a clear plan of action. Frame, prioritize, synthesize: this is your area of excellence.
  • Outcome direction: You like to follow a project to the end and measure what has been accomplished.
  • Relationship strength: You know how to hire various interlocutors, even without a hierarchical link. Your ability to federate makes a difference.
  • Curiosity & agility: You learn quickly, you adapt, and a subject you don't know is a subject you want to understand.
  • Rigor & pragmatism : You combine monitoring requirements and flexibility in execution.

YOUR CANDIDATE EXPERIENCE WITH US

We invite you to apply directly online. If your profile is selected, we will contact you again for a first HR telephone exchange.

Post based in Neuchâtel, with trips to Switzerland mainly.
