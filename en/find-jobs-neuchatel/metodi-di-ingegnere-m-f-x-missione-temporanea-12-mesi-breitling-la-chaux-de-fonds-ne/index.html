Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. We are a team of 12 employees and are currently looking for our next colleague to complete our #Squad! It is more than just a Engineer Methods position, it is a position in which you can put all your technical knowledge to use to actively participate in the improvement, optimization and development of our industrial processes. Your main mission will be to ensure the mastery, standardization, optimization and documentation of industrial processes to ensure the operational performance and robustness of manufacturing processes. You will intervene mainly in technical support for production, particularly in the context of industrialization, continuous improvement and document management of processes.

Your contribution

  • You define, industrialise and optimize the processes and methods of manufacturing and assembling watch movements and their subsets.
  • You optimize and standardize working methods to ensure the robustness, quality and performance of industrial processes, both within and with our external partners.
  • You perform and standardize the programs associated with production equipment according to industrial needs.
  • You define and maintain the production operating ranges and associated technical data.
  • You take time and ensure that production standards are maintained.
  • You prepare, update and maintain work instructions and operating standards in collaboration with the relevant departments.
  • You are involved in process and equipment validations and industrial qualifications, in collaboration with the Quality Department.
  • You are involved in projects to acquire, industrialise and integrate new means of production.
  • You are involved in digitalisation projects and in improving industrial tools.
  • You are actively involved in problem solving and continuous improvement (QRQC, technical analyses, corrective actions).
  • You capitalize, structure and transmit technical know-how related to industrial processes.
  • On request, you will participate in the training and integration of new employees on the method and process aspects.
  • You ensure appropriate, synthetic and structured communication with the various interlocutors.
  • You perform the different tasks in accordance with the working instructions and bring up to your hierarchy any drifts and problems encountered.
  • You contribute, as required, to any other mission or task entrusted by your hierarchy within the function. You think there's even more to find out about this job? You're right! Apply today to learn more! You will integrate perfectly with our team if:
  • You have been trained as a Process Management Engineer, in microtechnology, or as an equivalent.
  • You have 3 to 5 years of experience in a technical environment related to microtechnology, mechanics or watchmaking.
  • You have field experience in an industrial environment.
  • You master the principles of Lean Manufacturing and continuous improvement, including VSM, PDCA, 8D and DMAIC tools.
  • You have excellent analysis and problem solving capabilities.
  • You master the usual computer tools, including the Suite Office, as well as Inventor and Vault software.
  • You know the methods of industrialization and standardization of processes in the watchmaking field.
  • You are comfortable in an industrial environment and are able to observe, analyze the terrain and propose relevant and structured improvements.
  • You are able to work efficiently with production, quality, maintenance and industrialization teams.
  • You have perfect command of French, both oral and written.
  • You have good knowledge of English; any other linguistic knowledge is an asset.
  • You are a committed, supportive person with a true team spirit.
  • You are methodical, conscientious and rigorous in your work.
  • You are able to work independently while enjoying team collaboration.
  • You are creative in the search for solutions and know how to be a force for proposals. We invite you to apply even if you do not meet all of these criteria.
