Full description

Reference code: JR131708

Found in Florence in 1860, Panerai forged its reputation by developing precision instruments and professional watches for the Royal Italian Navy, renewed for their reliability and functional design. Rooted in performance, purpose, and precision, the Maison embraces its heritage while pushing the boundaries of innovation, combining Italian design with Swiss expertise. Today, Panerai continues to create tool watches that serve as trusted companions for every adventure, even in the most demanding environments.

As a future watchmaker, you will play a key role in the assembly and development of our watches. With your expertise and quality requirements, you will help ensure the accuracy, reliability and performance that make our House famous.

Your Impact

  • Assemble Haute Horlogerie watches until finished product.
  • Make adjustments and develop movements to optimize their performance.
  • Perform technical and aesthetic checks to ensure the conformity of each part.
  • Ensure the packing and self-control operations according to the standards of the House.
  • Ensure that quality requirements are met throughout the assembly process.
  • Identify technical issues and contribute to their resolution.
  • Actively participate in the continuous improvement of the methods and processes of the workshop.

Contribute to the department's performance, quality and reliability objectives.

Your profile

  • Holder of an CFC of watchmaker, watchmaker or equivalent training.
  • Mastering the techniques of adjustment and watchmaking.
  • Have good knowledge of Haute Horlogerie movements.
  • Demonstrate a high degree of thoroughness, rigour and a high sense of quality.
  • Work independently while cultivating a strong team spirit.
  • Demonstrate analytical capabilities and a constant willingness to advance and improve existing ones.

Why join us?

At Panerai, you will evolve within a dynamic and international environment, guided by a common passion for innovation and precision. We value the diversity of profiles, perspectives and experiences, putting each one's strengths at the service of collective success. We offer opportunities for continuous learning and development, so that everyone can build a successful career in Panerai.

Your journey with us

After selecting your application, our Human Resources team will contact you for a first exchange. During the process, you will discuss with the position manager, key partners and members of our management team.

Your next adventure starts at Panerai. We look forward to hearing from you.
