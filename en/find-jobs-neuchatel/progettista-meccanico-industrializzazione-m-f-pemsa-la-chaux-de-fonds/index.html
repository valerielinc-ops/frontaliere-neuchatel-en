Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of a fixed employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of (M/F) Mechanical Designer / Industrialization

Your assignment

  • Analyze technical requirements and develop appropriate solutions.
  • Design products and equipment with 3D CAD.
  • Making technical drawings, distinct base and production documentation.
  • Define and optimize production lines.
  • Collaborate closely with methods, CNC programming and production teams.
  • Participate in the stages of prototyping and pre-series.
  • Ensure technical monitoring until industrial validation.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of products and processes.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Higher education in mechanical design, industrialization or microtechnics.
  • Proven experience of several years in a precision processing environment.
  • Excellent knowledge of CNC processes and problems related to the production of complex components.
  • Master of 3D CAD software (SolidWorks is an advantage).
  • Experience is appreciated in the fields of watchmaking, jewellery or microtechnical.
  • Analytical spirit, rigor and sense of organization.
  • Ease of dealing with workshops and various technical departments.
