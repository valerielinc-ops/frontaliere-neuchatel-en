The “Integrated and Wireless Systems” division , based in Neuchâtel, Switzerland is looking for

Your mission

Proactively support the Business Unit’s employees on administrative topics, to facilitate their daily work.

Your responsibilities

  • Manage invoices, purchase orders, and expense reports.
  • Support the annual financial closing process, including invoices, accruals, and open purchase orders.
  • Monitor and verify time reporting records through Timelead.
  • Coordinate business travel arrangements and logistics.
  • Organize and schedule recruitment interviews.
  • Support the onboarding of new employees and maintain headcount records.
  • Provide administrative assistance to the Business Unit Manager and Deputy Manager.
  • Coordinate customer visits and ensure related logistics are in place.
  • Maintain and update customer information in the CRM, including contacts and mailing lists.
  • Order business cards and manage office-related administrative requests.
  • Handle import and export shipments and coordinate parcel deliveries.
  • Manage office supplies and ensure the availability of day-to-day consumables.
  • Collaborate closely with colleagues and contribute to the smooth running of administrative activities.
  • Scheduling, organization and coordination of various meetings, travels and events
  • Coordination of agendas of the management team

Your profile Know-how

  • Commercial Degree and a minimum of 2-3 years of experience in a similar function within an international environment.
  • Fluent in French and English (both oral and written), very good knowledge of German is a plus
  • Proficient with MS Office & MS Teams (SAP would be an asset)

Interpersonal skills

  • Organized, reliable and customer-oriented
  • Strong communication skills as well as excellent writing skills and enjoy working with figures
  • Proactive and multitasking
  • Well-developed team spirit while being able to work autonomously.
