Role overview

The manufacturing sector of the HR Pharmacy ServiceNever begin a significant technical and organizational transformation phase. In this development context, our department wants to strengthen its production team.

Your missions

  • Supporting the pharmacist responsible for conducting the manufacturing sector
  • Supervising the routine activities of the manufacturing sector: cytostatic, aseptic and non-sterile magistral preparations
  • Participating in the monitoring of the infrastructure and equipment constituting the technical environment of the sector
  • Participating in the maintenance and development of the documentary and d systems, information related to the manufacturing sector

Your profile

  • Federal diploma in pharmacists or title deemed equivalent by MEBE Ko
  • Authorization to practise independently
  • FPH title in hospital pharmacy or considered equivalent
  • Experience in the field of pharmaceutical manufacturing and knowledge of GMP
  • Experience in production under philanthropy and knowledge of the desired CATO software
  • Good mastery of the usual computer tools
