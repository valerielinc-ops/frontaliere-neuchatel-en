Full description

A member of the management committee of the Locle branch, your mission will be to pilot, structure and evolve all the activities of development of complicated movements.

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Organize and validate development activities and contribute actively to the definition of the overall strategy for movements, technical innovation and industrial performance
  • Orienting the teams towards a permanent monitoring of technological and watchmaking developments, both on movement architectures and on industrial materials, processes and means
  • Defining prototyping and development of high-complication movements, integrating the challenges of industrialisation and production reliability from the upstream
  • Contribute to the development and validation of technical and industrial feasibility studies in response to internal needs and strategic directions
  • Ensure respect for the development flow and the close link between the parent company and all the departments concerned (Product, Production, Quality, Methods, Procurement, Supply Chain) in a cross-cutting coordination role
  • Ensuring cost, timeliness, industrial robustness and technical reliability of developments
  • Ensure proper coordination of digitalization projects related to your perimeter
  • Structure and list all technical data on projects, ensure their preservation and ensure the management of intellectual property and patents within your scope
  • A Master in Engineering (EPF or equivalent), you have ideally completed your training with continuous training in management, leadership and/or business management
  • You have a significant and recognized experience in the development of watchmaking movements, including complicating movements, acquired within a High Watchmaking House, ideally in a function of responsibility
  • You justify a solid experience in managing complex projects, integrating development and industrialisation issues
  • Organised and proactive in nature, you are involved and know how to anticipate risks and make the decisions necessary to ensure the completion of projects in a demanding environment
  • With excellent communication skills, you can unite, engage and develop multidisciplinary teams of professional experts.
  • You have a good level of English (minimum B2) and master the desktop tools of the Windows environment
  • You are willing to travel regularly on our Brassus site

What we offer
