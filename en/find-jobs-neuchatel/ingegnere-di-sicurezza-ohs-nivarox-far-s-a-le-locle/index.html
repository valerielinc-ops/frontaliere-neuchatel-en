  • Your Mission

You are the technical reference for the safety of industrial processes and production equipment, with a particular focus on chemical hazards. Identify, assess, control and manage the risks associated with processes, chemicals and machinery. Actively contribute to regulatory compliance, continuous improvement of working conditions and the development of a pragmatic and operational safety culture. Main responsibilities Conduct risk analyses on all industrial processes and installations (precious metal refining, galvanoplasty, surface treatment) and on production equipment (manufacture, control and assembly machines). Piloting the safety of processes and installations: defining risk control measures, technical and organisational barriers, action plans and monitoring their implementation. Ensure the integrity of technical safety records of production equipment in collaboration with engineering. Ensure compliance of machinery and production equipment throughout the life cycle (acquisition or development, modification, maintenance) in accordance with regulatory requirements. Develop and develop job descriptions, secure operating procedures and procedures for the management of hazardous products: storage, handling, incompatibilities, retention and emergency situations.

  • Identify chemical hazards, assess exposure and accident scenarios and define appropriate prevention measures.
  • Supporting workshops in the implementation of good health and safety practices
  • Contribute to the investigation of accidents, incidents and pre-accidents and the implementation of corrective actions
  • Develop and conduct training and safety awareness activities

Ensure regulatory oversight and support industrial projects from the OSH perspective from the moment of design or modification of installations and make changes to our internal processes of equipment acquisition or development accordingly.

Profile

Engineering training in chemical engineering, industrial chemistry or equivalent technical advanced training. Additional training in safety, industrial hygiene or occupational health is an asset. Confirmed experience of at least 5 years in the industrial environment using hazardous chemicals, with significant experience in process safety and plant safety. Qualifications

  • Strong experience in process risk analysis and risk control measurement.
  • Good control of the principles of toxicology, occupational exposure and industrial ventilation, as well as the interpretation of exposure limit values.
  • Very good knowledge of the Swiss regulations applicable to safety at work and the environment as well as requirements for the storage and transport of dangerous goods.
  • Strong skills in managing and managing transversal projects, with the ability to animate multidisciplinary teams (production, maintenance, engineering, OSH, external providers).
  • Excellent relational and educational skills, with the ability to unite production teams and make safety a shared reflex.
