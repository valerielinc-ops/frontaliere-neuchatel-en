Full description

The plurality at work: a plus for your health Rue des Epancheurs 6, 2000 Neuchâtel Our diversity is our capital: CONCORDIA brings together people from different cultures, professions and areas of responsibility. Will you also enrich our team soon? For our agency in Neuchâtel, we are looking for versatile and open-minded people like you.

Your responsibilities

  • In a varied context, you advise by phone and face-to-face, you prospect new customers and sell health insurance and pension solutions tailored to their needs.
  • You independently and proactively manage the follow-up of existing insured persons as well as daily customer contacts.
  • You establish offers, perform administrative and fixed tasks appointments with potential customers.
  • Your ideas are welcome: you take part in actions at fairs or stands and represent CONCORDIA on site.

Your profile

  • You have completed basic vocational training in a sales-oriented environment and have a good understanding of the use of the computer.
  • You have excellent command of French, both oral and written – an essential asset for successful counseling.
  • Sales and advice are your passion: you have good contact with people and you can rely on an existing network that you are continuously developing.
  • Communication is your strong point: you have an assured attitude and you convince by your open and friendly way. Working independently and responsibly goes without saying to you.
  • You have completed AFA training or are motivated to follow it – we are accompanying you in this process.
