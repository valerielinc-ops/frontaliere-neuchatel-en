Full description

Your future playground You will join the Neuchâtel store, opened in 2017 (our first historic Decathlon store in Switzerland!). Ideally located in the mall of Marin-Epagnier, right next to Neuchâtel and close to the lake, it offers an exceptional working environment for sports enthusiasts. Your RUNNING perimeter is at the heart of our local strategy. Join an adventure that continues to grow Decathlon in Switzerland, it is a success story that started in 2017 in Neuchâtel. Since then, we have opened around ten stores a year to reach 50 outlets today. And we're not going to stop there! Our expansion continues, especially in German-speaking Switzerland, offering career opportunities throughout the country. Why is this job for you? You're looking for a moving profession, where you share your passion for sports from morning to night? You want to be proud to advise the right equipment and see your client go away with a smile? Do you like action, terrain and teamwork? Your 3 great benefits to us:

  • You're at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% in the field. You're Decathlon's face. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create the link.
  • You're inventing tomorrow's business: You're not just selling. You offer durable solutions: hardware rental, second-life buyback or digital orders. You're acting for sport and the planet.
  • You are autonomous and responsible: It's your ray! You manage your inventory, you highlight your novelties and you organize your business operations. Your impact is immediately apparent on your customers' satisfaction. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • The advice is: You welcome, you listen and you direct the athletes to the perfect product, whether on the shelf or through our digital tools. You also offer our loyalty program and manage the cash flow.
  • An impeccable ray: You implant the products, manage the "facing" and make your sales space attractive, dynamic and adapted according to the seasons.
  • Stock control: From the reception of the pallets to the dispatch, you guarantee that the customer always finds what he is looking for. You're also preparing web orders.
  • Circular economy: You are the ambassador of our new models, rental and second-hand purchase to offer a second life to equipment for example.
  • Specific missions: Depending on your wishes, you can become a reference on a topic, trainer for your colleagues or even help with the overall management of the store. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather sit behind a screen all day in a quiet place.
  • You don't like to go spontaneously to others.
  • You think unpacking pallets, tidying a radius and dealing with merchandise "that's not your problem."
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity (even if we communicate them to you 6 weeks in advance so that you can organize!).
