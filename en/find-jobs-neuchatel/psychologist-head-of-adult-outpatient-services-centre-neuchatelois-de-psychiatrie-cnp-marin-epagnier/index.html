Full description

The NPC recruits an Adult Ambulatory Psychologist. A stimulating environment and attractive working conditions await you.

Tasks

  • Organize and manage the outpatient psychology service.
  • Conduct psychological and psychotherapeutic treatments.
  • Train and supervise mental health professionals.

Competences

  • Diploma in psychology with specialization in psychotherapy required.
  • Excellent relational and communication skills.
  • Collaborative leadership and strategic vision.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The Medical Directorate is seeking, for its adult department at its ambulance service, a:

Psychologist Responsible 80% adult department, outpatient service

Your Mission

As a responsible Psychologist, together with the physician in charge of Ambulatory Service, you play a central role in the quality, coherence and development of psychological and psychotherapeutic care within your unit, sector or sector. Both experienced clinician, supervisor and leader, you actively contribute to the evolution of practice, professional training and clinical pilotage, in close cooperation with medical supervision.

Your responsibilities

Organization, leadership and development

  • Participate in the definition, implementation and monitoring of clinical and organizational objectives defined by the management of the NPC and the department.
  • Under medical delegation, conduct, organize and manage the service.
  • Plan activities to ensure quality of care, continuity of care and working conditions favourable to teams.
  • Carry out specific mandates and pilot projects.

Clinical activity

  • Develop and maintain direct and continuous clinical practice with patients.
  • Conduct clinical assessments, psychological reviews and therapeutic indications.
  • Conduct integrated psychotherapeutic treatments and counselling.
  • Work closely with the internal and external interdisciplinary network.
  • Participate in clinical reflection times, interdisciplinary meetings and departmental orientations.

Training, supervision and outreach

  • Actively participate in post-graduate and continuing training of psychologists, doctors and other professionals in care.
  • Provide oversight and knowledge transfer roles.
  • Contribute, according to your interests, to the influence of the institution (publications, conferences, participation in professional societies or commissions).

Your profile

  • University degree or HES in psychology.
  • Post-graduate title in psychotherapy of a recognized orientation, clinical psychology, neuropsychology or psychology of children and adolescents (for at least 5 years).
  • Experience confirmed for several years as a psychologist-psychotherapy.
  • Prior experience in supervision and training.
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss and cantonal health care system.
  • Use with common computer tools.
  • Collaborative leadership and ability to delegate.
  • Strategic vision and sense of planning.
  • Excellent relational, pedagogical and communication skills.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis, creativity and adaptability.
  • A strong sense of ethics, discretion and teamwork.

We offer you

  • A role with high clinical, managerial and institutional added value.
  • A stimulating interdisciplinary environment, at the heart of a recognised public institution.
  • The possibility of actively contributing to the development of mental health care, training and practices.
  • Working conditions governed by the applicable CTC, with attractive social benefits.
  • A privileged working environment in the canton of Neuchâtel, between lake and mountains.

The LWOP provides financial and logistical support in case of relocation to the canton (subject to conditions).
