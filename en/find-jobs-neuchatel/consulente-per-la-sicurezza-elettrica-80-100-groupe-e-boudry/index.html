Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Want to combine autonomy, diversity and technical expertise? As an Electrical Safety Advisor, you perform a variety of facility checks in the Boudry area and help ensure their compliance and safety. You are evolving in an environment that values trust, initiative and flexibility.

Electrical Safety Advisor 80-100%

Mission

  • Planning, organising, carrying out final facility controls in collaboration with the heads/projects, on an ad hoc basis periodic inspections
  • Compliance of installations in compliance with the Ordinance on ITTO low voltage electrical installations
  • Establish safety reports and protocols
  • Maintain the deadlines for facility notices, control reports and fault removal notices
  • Apply existing standards, ensure and continuously ensure that all safety measures are applied and taken into account in the workplace to ensure the health of employees

Profile

  • The Federal Master's Office, supplemented by a Federal Patent for Electrical Safety Adviser, is an asset.
  • Ideally a few years of experience as an Electrical Safety Advisor
  • Organised and autonomous, you have a sense of anticipation and responsibility
  • You are recognized as an open minded, flexible and autonomous person.
  • Enjoy contact and have a good sense of customer service
  • You have the cat driver's license. B
