Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Department of Childhood and Adolescence is seeking, for the complex intensive outpatient service (AIC), a

Assistant Psychologist 40%

The Department of Children and Adolescents is responsible for the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of children and adolescents aged 0-18. The position is affiliated with the complex intensive outpatient service (AIC) at the institution "The Carrefour Foundation" within the ASAEF (Accompaniment and Ambulatory Support to the Child and his Family). You are a psychologist with a master's degree in psychology recognized by PsyCo and are in post-graduate training in psychotherapy.

Your main missions

  • Clinical evaluations and intensive multidisciplinary care in the family environment, in order to enhance access to existing resources in the family and encourage a change in the functioning of the family and social system in which the child is inserted and developed.
  • Regular and active collaboration with the network, including the Child Protection Office.
  • Work in a multidisciplinary team (psychologists, educators, social workers).
  • Participate in networking with families, schools, paediatricians and other partners (returns, exchanges, coordination).
  • Participate in team meetings, supervision and institutional development time.

Skills and profile sought

Essential

  • To hold a university master's degree in psychology or equivalent recognized by PsyCo.
  • Be in post-graduate training in psychotherapy, or confirmation of enrolment obtained.
  • Ability to work as a multidisciplinary team and to collaborate constructively.
  • Be in possession of a driver's licence and a vehicle.

Cross-cutting competencies

  • Autonomy.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis.
  • Relationship qualities: communication, listening, professional discretion.
  • Clinical curiosity, rigour and ability to manage professional emergency when necessary.
  • Mastery of French and common computer tools.
