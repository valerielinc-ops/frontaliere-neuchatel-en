CONTEXT

Within the production department of our Manufacture Joaillere located in the Locle and more specifically of our workshop in charge of the manufacture of components, we are looking for a decal to perform adjustments and gears on CNC decals, in accordance with the specifications and deadlines required.

WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

You make sure to mount, adjust and start production machines of Citizen types (L12, M16). You are responsible for optimising and supervising the production and control of the components.

YOUR MISSION

REGISTRATION AND TRAINING OF CNC COLLECTIONS

  • You check the compliance of input data: tools received, plans, machine programs, control matrix
  • You set up the right tools: possible dismantling of the tools of the previous production and then installing the new tools
  • You adjust the decal with possible program development and tool setting.
  • You provide the necessary information for the production follow-up to your Head
  • You participate in the definition of the machining strategy with the internalization services

MONITORING PRODUCTION AND QUALITY

  • You take samples during production in order to carry out the required checks (visual and dimensional) in accordance with the control plans and via the use of measuring instruments (control of profiles by optical measurement, comparison, gauges)
  • You record the control data according to the applicable procedure.
  • You guarantee conformity of current production by correcting deviations if necessary
  • You notice quality failures, stop production in order to perform tool change or defuse.
  • You re-conform the piece after changing tools (respect for ribs and tolerances)
  • You restart the production and ensure the supply bar supply

PARTICIPATING IN THE MAINTENANCE OF EQUIPMENT

  • You are involved in machine maintenance by performing tasks related to first level maintenance: oil level, machine cleanliness, etc.
