Full description

Within our manufacturing and decoration workshops, you will be responsible for guaranteeing the optimal progress of production, compliance with delivery times for Manufacturing Orders and the efficient scheduling of all operations. As a true pivot between planning, the workshop and logistics, you ensure the fluidity of the production flow and the reliability of system data. Your responsibilities Scheduling & production management

  • Manage the overall workshop planning and assign work to employees and/or machines.
  • Create, optimize and monitor the weekly production plan for a fleet of approximately 15 machines.
  • Prioritize and adjust OFs in close collaboration with Planning, workshop managers and adjusters.
  • Update and monitor the end-of-production schedule (e.g. Gantt chart).
  • Announce and manage delays, in conjunction with customer workshops and logistics.
  • Replace, if absent and if necessary, the assembly scheduler. Management of OF & data accuracy
  • Carry out all administrative entries and recordings in the ERP
  • Modify the ranges to add the necessary operations (e.g. NQ retouches).
  • Ensure consistency between system data (scheduled times, operations, etc.) and reality on the ground.
  • Guarantee seizures linked to requests for parts entering the department. Administrative support & coordination
  • Provide administrative support to managers.
  • Collaborate with teams to streamline information flows. Performance analysis & continuous improvement
  • Monitor workshop performance indicators (KPI).
  • Identify deviations, propose corrective actions and participate in process optimizations.
  • Be a force of proposal in a logic of continuous improvement.
  • You have at least 5 years of experience as a workshop manager in a bar turning or machining workshop and you have knowledge of decoration.
  • You are proficient in Word and Excel.
  • You are used to working with usual industrial performance indicators and are able to make decisions based on them. Knowledge of M3 is a plus.
  • Methodical and orderly person, you have good analytical skills and are ready to assume responsibilities. Your proactivity and excellent interpersonal skills allow you to achieve the objectives set.
  • You demonstrate autonomy in organizing your work and you have a strong ability to work in a team and communicate at different levels. What we offer:
  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 weeks of annual leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sporting or cultural activities
  • Possibility of working remotely from several sites (GE/VD/NE)
  • A LPP with advantageous rates
