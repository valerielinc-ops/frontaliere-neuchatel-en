Group leader, Electricians - Swatch Group

Group leader, Electricians Introduction of society Comadur SA, World leader in the production of Swiss Made hard materials components. We are part of the Swatch Group, the world's leading watchmaker. Recognized as the preferred partner of the Group's brands for the production of Ceramics, Sapphire Glaces and Clock Stones for the watch industry. We are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams with the help of their talents, ambitions and inspirations. As part of our growth and development, we offer a position at the Col-des-Roches site: Group Manager, Electricians (M/F)
