Full description

An institution governed by cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. Any designation of person or function shall be understood equally between male and female. The Directorate of Care is seeking to complete its paramedical team within the child psychiatry hospital unit (H1), one:

Psychomotorian

As part of your function you will be required to

  • carrying out psychomotor assessments in the triple perspective: prevention, rehabilitation and therapy
  • assist in the overall care of children and adolescents in hospital units for mental disorders
  • set up body-based therapies using various techniques such as games, body expression, relaxation, etc.
  • carry out its activities in conjunction with the other members of the team under the medical-care responsibility

Your required skills are

  • hold a diploma in psychomotority HES (or a diploma deemed equivalent)
  • benefit from several years of experience in a psychiatric care service for childhood and adolescence
  • know how to work in a multidisciplinary framework
  • demonstrate rigour and analytical and synthesis capacity
  • be endowed with a common relational sense and a listening and communication capacity
  • have a sense of responsibility and initiative and decision-making, be autonomous
  • be creative and enthusiastic

Entry into office: 1 September 2026 or to be agreed Place of work : Prefargier site – 2074 Marin-Epagnier, Contract Type: Undetermined Term Contract (IDC)

Prefecture 2074 The Tene
