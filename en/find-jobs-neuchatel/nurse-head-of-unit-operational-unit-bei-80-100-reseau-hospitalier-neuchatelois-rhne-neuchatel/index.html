Full description

Our operating unit offers a sophisticated and efficient multidisciplinary technical platform with eleven multi-site operating rooms at La Chaux-de-Fonds and Pourtalès sites. On a daily basis, it is a dynamic and highly specialized team that takes care of patients who are expected to receive planned or emergency surgery.

Nurse Head of Unit Operational Unit at 80-100%

Your missions

  • Direct and supervise a team of 80 employees at the Pourtalès (Neuchâtel) site
  • Manage and coordinate the organisation of the operations of the operating unit comprising the IDDO team, TSO and ATSSO
  • Ensure efficient patient care and quality of care in accordance with existing protocols and recommendations
  • Collaborate with its counterpart at La Chaux-de-Fonds to ensure organizational coherence and harmonization of practices between the two operating units
  • Ensuring the application of principles of professional ethics and the dissemination of care values
  • Functional supervision of teams, support for new arrivals and the development of skills within its department (continuous training)
  • Organize the business sector in an optimal way in collaboration with the different partners.
  • Participate in the overall management of the operating block with partners

Your profile

  • Diploma in nursing HES (or equivalent recognized CRS) with specialization in operating unit (IDDO)
  • Authorization to practise a health profession in the canton of Neuchâtel
  • Work experience of at least 3 years as an instrumentalist
  • Management training, community management or willingness to attend training
  • Team management experience, an asset

Your skills

  • Ability to lead, manage and unite a team
  • A sense of responsibility and natural leadership
  • Relationship skills in communication and conflict management
  • Teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Sense of priorities, ability to manage the unexpected, and its stress
  • Be proposal strength, flexible and available
  • Common software knowledge one plus (Outlook, Carefolio, POLYPOINT, Opale)
