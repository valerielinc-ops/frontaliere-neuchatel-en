Full description

Reference code: JR134109

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

As early as February 2027 and for a period of six months, we are looking for a trainee to work on modeling the exhaust of a mechanical watch.

LIABILITY

In direct collaboration with the Quality, Performance & Reliability team and the watch laboratory, under the responsibility of your trainee master, your missions will be as follows:

  • Bibliographical research: basic movement, exhaust theory;
  • Understanding, ownership and improvement of existing models;
  • Digital simulation taking into account its environment;
  • Sensitivity analysis of the most critical parameters;
  • Comparison tests/calculations and numerical calibration;
  • Preparation of a detailed report explaining the results and approach;
  • Communication of results within the team and the Manufacture.

PROFILE

  • Student engineer EPF, Grandes Écoles or equivalent, Master level, in computer science and engineering, mechanical engineering, mathematics or equivalent.
  • Specialization in modeling, numerical methods or finite elements.
  • Excellent knowledge in programming (python, matlab), modelling (Ansys) and optimization.
  • Curious, dynamic, organized and autonomous, you have a good spirit of synthesis and are the force of proposal.
  • Equipped with a good handler, you show editorial ease and scientific rigor.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience within a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in Buttes, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait any longer, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!
