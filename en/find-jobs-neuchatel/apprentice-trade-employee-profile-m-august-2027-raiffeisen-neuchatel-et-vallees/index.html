31.08.2026 Neuchâtel and Vallées

Be part of our team!

Do you attach importance to solid basic training? Are you a motivated, responsible person who likes to communicate and who is interested in the banking world? Don't hesitate and apply to join our team!

What are you waiting for?

During your three years of training, supervised by experienced and motivated trainers, you will discover the different fields of activity and become familiar with the daily life of the Bank Raiffeisen Neuchâtel and Vallées. In addition, you will study banking theory and techniques at the CYP (Centre de compétences professionnel pour les banques) in Lausanne. You will carry out internships in the agencies of our department (Neuchatelian Littoral, Val-de-Travers, Val-de-Ruz).

What are you bringing?

  • You have successfully completed your compulsory education or are currently in the final year with good or even very good grades;
  • You have an interest in banking and human contact;
  • You are willing to acquire professional knowledge and social skills;
  • You are motivated and appreciate teamwork;
  • Your home is within the bank's business line.
  • a cover letter;
  • a resume;
  • the academic results of your last four semesters.

Build your future where trust, proximity and team spirit make a difference. Join the Bank Raiffeisen Neuchâtel and Vallées and give your learning a concrete dimension, enriching and full of opportunities. Lou Pessotto 3rd year apprenticeship Why Raiffeisen?

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We live in diversity and equal opportunities

We encourage diversity of perspectives, experiences and skills within our teams. This diversity makes us rich and makes a decisive contribution to our success.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, through a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.
