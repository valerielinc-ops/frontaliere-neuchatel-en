Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our clients realize their projects by providing global, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether for the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the balance between private and professional life.

A local player, Groupe E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, panels and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as the maintenance of installations.

For our company Elexpert SA and in order to carry out checks of electrical installations for a varied clientele made up of individuals, businesses, management, industries and public authorities, we are looking for:

Electrical safety advisor 80-100%

Mission

As an independent inspection body, we contribute every day to the safety of people and property by verifying the conformity of electrical installations according to the legal and normative requirements in force.

Your responsibilities

  • Independently carry out periodic inspections, reception and site inspections according to the ITTO in the region of Neuchâtel, Jura and Jura Bernois.
  • Check the conformity of electrical installations and identify possible non-conformities.
  • Establish control reports, measurement protocols and safety reports.
  • Inform owners, operators and installers of the defects observed and the corrective measures to be implemented.
  • Ensure, in collaboration with the secretariat, the administrative monitoring of files until their closure.
  • Advise customers on questions relating to electrical safety and current regulations.
  • Participate in developing the quality of services and maintaining a high level of technical competence.
  • Maintain relationships of trust with various partners in the sector.

Profile

  • CFC of electrician installer, electrician planner or equivalent training, supplemented by a Federal Certificate of Electrical Safety Advisor.
  • Experience in the control of electrical installations and solid experience in the field of electrical installation.
  • Excellent knowledge of current standards and requirements (ITBT, NIBT).
  • Ability to work independently and organized.
  • Writing skills for establishing technical reports.
  • Sense of service, diplomacy and excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Category B driving license.
  • Recent graduates are welcome and will benefit from personalized support to take on this new challenge.

What we offer you

  • An evolving environment favorable to balance between professional and private life.
  • The social benefits of a modern company, fair treatment, professional development opportunities.
  • Integration into a team with extensive experience.
  • Continuing training each year.
  • Service vehicle.
  • Possibility of organizing your schedule according to different requirements.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: “Together, we are moving towards success”.
