Full description

Reference code: JR134120

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

As early as February 2027 and for a period of 6 months, we are looking for a trainee for the conduct of work aimed at establishing new manufacturing strategies using laser micromachining equipment.

LIABILITY

  • System optimization: To understand and optimize the parameters of a complex opto-mechanical machine.
  • Materials Science & Process: Study light-matter interaction on a micrometric scale. You will propose and validate innovative machining strategies to ensure the material integrity and surface condition of the parts.
  • Experimentation & Vulgarization: Design experimental plans (DoE), carry out test campaigns, analyse the results and ensure their restitution.
  • Proposition force & Innovation: You will be able to propose new ways of improving, whether in terms of productivity, geometric quality or design freedom.

PROFILE

  • Student in Microtechnics, Mechatronics, Mechanical, Physical or Equivalent (Master level);
  • You have a strong taste for practical machine experimentation combined with a rigorous scientific approach;
  • Knowledge of femtosecond optics and/or multi-axis trajectory programming are important assets;
  • Good communication and outreach capabilities;
  • Curious.se, creative.ve, autonomous and power of proposal;
  • Taste for teamwork and the desire to learn.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience in a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in Couvet, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!
