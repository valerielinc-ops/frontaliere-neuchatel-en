Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

YOUR MISSION

Implement a global security culture within the site.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

Ensuring human health and safety

  • Pilot, deploy and analyse the process of identifying risks at the workplace
  • Propose a progress plan consistent with the company's strategic, tactical and operational objectives
  • Ensuring regulatory compliance of the site
  • Build the training modules and lead the training of all employees on health risks and accidents (external accidents).
  • Pilot and participate in carrying out OSH audits in the company.
  • Framework external EHSS approaches
  • Establish a system of traceability of accidents, incidents and hazardous situations. Supervise the implementation of the necessary corrective measures.
  • Monitoring and ensuring progress of action plans related to human health (risk management, audits, incidents, spontaneous referrals).
  • Develop and disseminate a global HSE culture aimed at reducing accidents/diseases/diseases associated with the workplace or in the extra-professional setting.
  • Set up and control the organization in the event of an emergency (detection, reaction, evacuation, fire case, support management in crisis management).
  • Supervise the on/off boardings of the employees (badges access, training / basic information etc.).

Ensuring the security of persons and property

  • Work closely with lodge officers
  • Develop and maintain a safety concept (yc installations) based on manufacturing activities and group standards and manage the overall dissemination of existing rules and employee training.
  • Manage the break-in system and access control system, access to safes, video surveillance.

Ensure our carbon footprint is reduced.

  • Conduct environmental analysis according to the group's methodology and propose an environmental policy consistent with our entire ecosystem.
  • Implement waste monitoring and propose reduction solutions.
  • To guarantee compliance with environmental protection standards.
  • Ensure contact with competent authorities. (SENE, commune).
  • Maintain the list of chemicals and propose alternatives.
  • Pilot and monitor environmental indicators (waste, VOC.) and energy consumption.
  • Offer optimizations of our installations (HVAC, compressed air...)

Activity management

  • Lead management committees
  • Attend external meetings within the group

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • Relationship strength
  • Impacting oral communication
  • Good editorial level
  • Spirit of synthesis and federator
