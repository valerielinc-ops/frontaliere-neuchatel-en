Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of a fixed employment? For 30 years we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, proposing them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of (M/F) scaffolding squad

Your assignment

  • Supervision and coordinate scaffolding mounts in the yards.
  • Participate actively in the assembly, modification and disassembly of the structures.
  • Organize daily work and distribute tasks within the team.
  • Check compliance with safety standards and SUVA directives.
  • Check plant compliance and carry out testing checks.
  • Ensure material monitoring and report any needs.
  • Collaboration with the leaders, customers and various stakeholders involved in the construction site.
  • Form and support new employees and apprentices.
  • To draft construction reports and ensure administrative follow-up on interventions.

And now we're talking about you?

  • CFC Diploma of scaffolding or proven experience in a similar task
  • At least 5 years of experience in the field of scaffolding
  • Experience in managing a team
  • Excellent mastery of scaffolding techniques (side, roofs, suspended, modular)
  • Good knowledge of safety standards and regulations in force
  • Essential driving licence
  • At ease in work at altitude
  • Autonomous, organized and equipped with a true leadership
