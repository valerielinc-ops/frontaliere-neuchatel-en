Role overview

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment.

As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Verify the conformity of electrical installations and identify any non-compliances.
  • Prepare control reports, measurement protocols and safety reports.
  • Inform owners, operators and installers of defects and corrective measures to be implemented.
  • In collaboration with the secretariat, ensure the administrative follow-up of files until their closure.
  • Advise customers on electrical safety issues and current requirements.
  • Participate in the development of the quality of services and the maintenance of a high level of technical competence.
  • Maintain a relationship of trust with the various partners in the sector. Profile:
  • SWC of installer-electrician, planner-electrician or equivalent training, supplemented by a Federal Patent for Electrical Security Advisor.
  • Experience in the control of electrical installations and solid experience in the field of electrical installation.
