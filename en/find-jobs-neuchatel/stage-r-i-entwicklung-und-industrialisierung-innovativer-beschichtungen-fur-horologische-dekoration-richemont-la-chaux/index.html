Full description

Reference code: JR134122

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

In this role, you will implement a solution on the development of innovative coatings for watchmaking decoration. These processes bring together different scientific and technical fields such as chemistry, materials science, vacuum deposition.

LIABILITY

In direct collaboration with the Materials, Chemistry and Surfaces team, under the responsibility of your internship tutor, your missions will be as follows:

  • Conducting the bibliographical search;
  • Design and implementation of experience plans;
  • Preparation of analytical and manufacturing methods;
  • Conduct of experimental studies and analysis of results;
  • Material characterization;
  • Performing metrological checks.

PROFILE

  • You are a student in engineering science, master level and you have good knowledge in chemistry and surface processing.
  • Vacuum deposit knowledge is a plus.
  • Able to carry out an experimental campaign and analyse its results, you have a special interest in watchmaking and innovation.
  • Dynamic, organized and autonomous, you have a good spirit of synthesis and are the force of proposition. You're a man of editorial convenience and analytical rigor.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience in a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment. •The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in La Chaux-de-Fonds in the premises of the Manufacture ProCadrans, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!
