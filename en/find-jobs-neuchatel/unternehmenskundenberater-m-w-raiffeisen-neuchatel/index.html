02.10.2026 Neuchâtel

Be part of our team!

The Bank Raiffeisen Neuchâtel and Vallées is a prosperous bank, firmly anchored in its region, with branches on the Littoral, Val-de-Ruz and Val-de-Travers. With a balance sheet of around CHF 2.4 billion and more than 95 collaborators, we are continuing our development in the interest of our members and customers, while strengthening our values as a sustainable and committed cooperative. In order to strengthen our customer team companies on the Littoral Neuchatelois, we are looking for an experienced customer advisor. Based in Colombia, you are actively involved in the development of our activities. You develop a portfolio of SME clients and are the Bank's focal point for the support of real estate companies and real estate developers, particularly in the context of their financing projects.

What are you waiting for?

  • You are the preferred interlocutor of our real estate customers and accompany him in his financing projects;
  • You advise and support a client company in all its banking needs;
  • You are actively developing your portfolio through the acquisition of new customers and the maintenance of your network;
  • You analyze, structure and present complex financing files in accordance with the Bank's regulatory requirements and quality standards;
  • You represent the Bank with the region's economic players and actively contribute to its development.

What are you bringing?

  • Recognized expertise in the financing of real estate companies and real estate promotions;
  • A solid experience of several years in consulting clients at a Swiss bank;
  • Excellent control of financial analysis and corporate financing;
  • A valid SAQ certification or the willingness to obtain it within the defined time limits;
  • A good knowledge of the Neuchâtel economic fabric and its actors;
  • An autonomous personality, committed and oriented solutions.

Why join us? We offer a rich and varied activity within a dynamic regional bank, benefiting from a strong local anchor and a real proximity to its customers. You will evolve in a modern work environment where responsibility, trust and collaboration are central. Undertaking, advising, advancing our clients' projects and contributing to the development of a dynamic regional bank: your next challenge starts here! Carole Vauthier Member of the Management, Head of Business Sector Why Raiffeisen?
