Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad in La Chaux-de-Fonds as a watchmaker, watchmaker and part of this extraordinary mission! We are currently looking for our next, next colleague to complete our #Squad! It's more than just a watchmaker position, it's a position where you can put all your knowledge to participate actively in the improvement and development of the activity. Your main mission will be to ensure the assembly of watch components, both on and off line, in the process of assembly of movements. You also perform the re-conformity operations as well as the chronometric settings, respecting the quality requirements and procedures in force.

Your contribution

  • You ensure the installation of watch components.
  • You do screwing using an electric screwdriver.
  • You perform oil and lubricating operations.
  • You perform the autonomous control of the parts (self-control).
  • You perform the chronometric settings.
  • You perform processing operations.
  • You perform the decotting operations.
  • You ensure reconditioning of the components.
  • You perform the different tasks in accordance with the work instructions and bring back to your hierarchy the drifts and problems encountered.
  • You actively participate in field animations: SQUADS, 5S, MM, etc.
  • You participate in the QRQC at the request of the person in charge.
