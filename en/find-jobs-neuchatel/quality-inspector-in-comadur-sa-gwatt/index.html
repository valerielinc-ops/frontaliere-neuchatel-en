Quality inspector,-in - Swatch Group

Quality inspector,-in Introduction of the société Comadur specializes in the production of components from high-quality hard materials such as sapphire, watch glass, ceramics and micromagnets. Our products are particularly used in the watch industry. For our location in Gwatt we are looking for a quality inspector (M/F). Description of post

  • Measuring, testing microcomponents with various measuring instruments, as well as microscope
  • Visual inspection and dimensional inspection of small parts
  • Drawing up and completing the production orders
  • Assistance in the optimization and further development of our QA processes
  • Support of the Head of Quality Assurance
  • Profile
  • Ideally completed apprenticeship, ideally in a technical profession
  • Professional experience in a similar function beneficial
  • Safe reading of drawings is advantageous
  • Basic knowledge of quality management systems is beneficial
  • Competence requirements
  • Independent, structured and goal-oriented working method
  • Self-responsible careful, exact and clean work
  • Flexible and resilient personality
  • Knowledge of ERP – systems are advantageous
  • Safe handling of MS-Office
