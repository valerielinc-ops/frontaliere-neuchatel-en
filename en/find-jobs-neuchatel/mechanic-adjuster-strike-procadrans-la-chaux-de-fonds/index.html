YOUR MISSION

Make the balance adjustment and set-up, ensure and guarantee the production of parts in terms of quality, quantity and deadlines for the different production lines. Your missions

Machinery adjustment

  • In line with scheduling, prepare the means of production.
  • Perform the balance adjustment.
  • Validate new tools.
  • Ensure settings and start with control of the first components.

Maintenance of production tools

  • Ensure production and quality monitoring of the parts produced (statistical checks).
  • Monitor the proper operation of the machine and participate in its preventive maintenance.
  • Manage the proper care of the pads.

Perform manufacturing operations

  • Achieve defined pace objectives.
  • Meet quality criteria.
  • Follow job instructions and working methodologies.
  • Alert in case of abnormal process or excessive waste.
  • Be autonomous in manufacturing and adjustment on the trades concerned.
  • Perform self-control operations on its production according to current quality standards.

Problem solving

  • Alert the trade specialist.
  • Be able to bring the first steps of analysis to a problem before alerting the person in charge (30) maximum.
  • Participate in problem solving if requested.

Continuous improvement

  • Encourage reflection with technical specialists.
  • Propose improvements to optimize performance.
  • Work and participate in projects in collaboration with internal partners.

Occupational Safety & Health - Safety - Environment

  • Respect the rules of its business and business in terms of safety, safety and respect for the environment.
  • Involved in accident prevention, safety and environmental respect.
  • Do not endanger his colleagues or yourself by his behaviour.
