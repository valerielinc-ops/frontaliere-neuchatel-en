Logistician Learning / Logistician CFC - Swatch Group

Logistician Learning / Logistician CFC Introduction of society For over 175 years our employees have developed and transmitted, from generation to generation, a microtechnical know-how unique in the art of aesthetics, miniaturization and precision. Description of post The Logistician is responsible for the storage and transfer of the goods, from their entry to their exit from the enterprise. He is the guarantor of the accuracy of the data of the stored equipment. To do so, he receives the items, controls their quantities, transports them and stores them in the appropriate locations and stores them in the computer system. It also makes them available and regularly monitors inventory status by conducting inventories.
