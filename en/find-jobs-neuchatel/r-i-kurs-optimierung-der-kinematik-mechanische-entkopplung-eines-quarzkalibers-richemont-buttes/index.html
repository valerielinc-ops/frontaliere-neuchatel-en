Full description

Reference code: JR134121

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

Want to participate in the creation of tomorrow's watches?

Join our Research & Innovation team for a 6 month internship starting in February 2027, in a stimulating and innovative environment.

LIABILITY

Throughout your internship, you will work closely with experts from various fields (construction, microtechnical, electromagnetic).

  • Your missions will be as follows:
  • State of art: search for innovative concepts
  • 3D design and complete CAD (Creo) movement modelling.

•Kinematic simulations (operating games, resistant couples).

•Planning and monitoring the manufacture of components

  • Micromechanical assembly, development and fine tuning of the prototype.
  • Functional characterization, performance measures, technical report & presentation to all R&I teams.

PROFILE

•Student in the final year of engineering school in Microtechnic or Mechanical Engineering, ideally with a specialization in watchmaking construction.

•Excellent mastery of 3D design under CAD (Creo).

•Passioned by micromechanical watchmaking, gear theory and clever mechanisms.

•Curious.se, creative.ve, autonomous and powerful proposal.

•Taste for teamwork and the desire to learn.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

•A unique experience within a prestigious international group.

•A stimulating and collaborative working environment.

•The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.

•A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in Buttes, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!
