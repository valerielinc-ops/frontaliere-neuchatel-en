Full description

Attached to the Head of Quality, your mission will be to drive the quality teams in charge of continuous improvement, the long-term resolution of quality issues in production and the securing of new products, from development to marketing, in a demanding and excellent approach. In this context, you will be in charge of:

  • Lead and develop a multidisciplinary team of 6 employees
  • Deploy the operational excellence strategy based on the principles of Lean Management and Kaizen culture for continuous improvement
  • Pilot projects of operational excellence
  • Identify areas for process improvement
  • Pilot activities related to the quality security of new products from development to the first year of life in markets
  • Overseeing the treatment of internal and external non-compliances through rigorous and structured approaches to managing quality issues
  • You have a higher degree in quality, engineering or equivalent and have at least 5 years of experience in a similar position
  • You demonstrate expertise in continuous improvement (Lean, Six Sigma) and are ideally certified green belt
  • You have strong knowledge of industrial quality and problem solving tools
  • You have a thorough knowledge of watchmaking processes
  • With a federative spirit, you show leadership and enjoy cross-cutting exchanges
  • You master the usual IT tools (ERP, MES, Excel, Power BI, project management) We look forward to receiving your application, which we will process as soon as possible. What we offer:
  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
