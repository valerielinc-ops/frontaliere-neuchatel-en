Production employees/self-testers(M/F) Mech-/Industry - Swatch Group

Production employees/self-testers(M/F) mech/industry Introduction of the société Comadur is the specialist in the production of components made of hard materials such as sapphire, ruby, ceramics and Micromagnets, which are mainly intended for the watch industry. For our location in Thun, we are looking for: Description of post

  • Independent operation of equipment (mechanical) in production
  • Resolving Occasional Disorders When Needed
  • Measuring, testing microcomponents with various measuring instruments, as well as microscope
  • Visual inspection and dimensional inspection of small parts
  • Independent production of tools and accessories
  • General mechanical work
  • Introducing creative ideas for process improvement

Profile

  • This production employee is well versed in fine motors and works in mechanical engineering.

Industrial environment

  • Several years of experience in the industrial environment are advantageous
  • Mechanical vocational training not mandatory, lateral entrants welcome
  • Experience in manufacturing mechanical parts of small size

Competence requirements

  • Good comprehension of mechanical processes and functions
  • Good sensitivity, good visual acuity (mainly small parts)
  • They are practice-oriented, can tackle and find solutions independently
  • You are a resilient, team-oriented personality

Langues

  • German in Word and Writing,
