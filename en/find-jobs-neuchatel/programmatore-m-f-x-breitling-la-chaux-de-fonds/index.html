Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad in La Chaux-de-Fonds as a Programmer Regulating, Programmer Regulating and part of this extraordinary mission! We are currently looking for our next, next colleague to complete our # Squad! It's more than just a Programmer Setter, it's a position in which you can put all your knowledge to participate actively in the improvement and development of the activity. Your main mission will be to regulate, program and produce watchpieces at CN-controlled machining centres, in small series and preseries, in accordance with deadlines and quality requirements. You will also be responsible for the maintenance of tools, positions and equipment to ensure the availability of the means of production.

Your contribution

  • You produce parts in small series (machining, washing and filling).
  • You set up the machining centers and ensure the launch of the preseries.
  • You program and optimize CN programs at machining centres.
  • You ensure the maintenance of the tools for the various production departments.
  • You perform dimensional control of parts produced using conventional measuring means.
  • You are involved in the in-process controls.
  • You perform first-level preventive and corrective maintenance on equipment.
  • You ensure a versatility within the machining workshop, according to the needs defined by your manager.
  • You are actively contributing to optimisation and activity development.
  • You perform the different tasks according to the work instructions and report to your hierarchy any drift or problems encountered.
  • You are participating in the continuous improvement process, proposing ideas and contributing to the implementation of the selected ideas.
