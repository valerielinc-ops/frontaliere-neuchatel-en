We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad in La Chaux-de-Fonds as a draftsman, technical designer and be part of this extraordinary mission! We are a team of 4 employees and are currently looking for our next, next colleague to complete our # Squad! You will be attached to the site of La Chaux-de-Fonds . It's more than just a draftsman's position, it's a position in which you can put all your knowledge to participate actively in the improvement and development of the activity. Your main mission will be to develop, update and distribute necessary operational plans to the various manufacturing departments in collaboration with the various project managers.

Your contribution

  • You are preparing the operational plan files for the various departments of the factory.
  • You monitor and update existing plan files, ensuring the quality and accuracy of technical data and basic data.
  • You provide proactive support to tool builders and Industrialization technicians, in particular by checking the coherence between the ranges and the different phases.
