We are looking for reinforcement for our disposal and recycling team at the Neuchâtel am Rhein site. Do you like to get involved, work reliably and quality-consciously and keep an overview in a dynamic logistics environment? Then you are exactly right with us.

Mansioni

  • Proper handling and disposal of hazardous waste
