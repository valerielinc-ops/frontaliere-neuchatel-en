Full description

Facing the challenges of our time Help us grow and be more impactful! Our dynamic and successful Business Unit dedicated to Micro & Nano Systems, based in Neuchâtel, Switzerland, is looking for a business-savvy, visionary and communicative Business Unit Leader.

Your responsibilities

Strategic

  • Define and deploy the strategy of the Business Unit in alignment with the company’s strategy
  • Proactively participate to the Executive Committee
  • Steer the relationship with local stakeholders (Canton NE, EPFL, Industry associations)
  • Define the research program and technology roadmap activities of the BU
  • Define the business development strategy and programs
  • Drive the business acquisition and ensure a good relationship with all internal and external partners
  • Set the BU Communication and Marketing activities in collaboration with the central Business Development and MARCOM team
  • Proactively support the internal collaboration with the other BU Leaders

Financial

  • Define, control and ensure the BU’s budget and financial reporting
  • Manage and approve the project portfolio and all BU investments
  • Drive and supervise the Microfabrication operations and Cleanroom strategic investments in concert with local stakeholders

Quality management/ISO

  • Ensure that the BU meets the company’s Quality standards (ISO Standards 9001 and 14001)
  • Supervise and ensure the Project delivery and Microfabrication quality commitment to clients
  • Supervise external audits and evaluate external audit reports, in collaboration with the Quality Managers

Human Resources/Management

  • Lead a BU of over 80 experts and engineers
  • Directly manage a team of 8 senior leaders (Deputy BU Leader, Group Leaders, Research, Business Development, Operations and Finance Managers)
  • Define annual goals and ensure the development of all employees at BU level, in particular the development of young talents
  • Maintain a motivating and balanced high-tech working environment
  • In collaboration with the VP Human Resources and HRBP, approve all hirings and promotions, and ensure the equity of salaries

Relationships

  • Responsible for the political networking and contracts in the Canton of Neuchatel and other institutional stakeholders

Your Profile Know-how

  • At least a Master’s degree in Engineering, PhD or MBA as an asset
  • International experience in high-tech industry is highly desirable
  • At least 10 years experience in senior management position in high-tech industry – Semiconductors, Electronics components & devices, Photonics, Micro Systems
  • High level overview and understanding of the global high-tech industry
  • Business acumen and understanding of industry macro trends
  • Good understanding of Micro Systems driven application domains as medical devices, electronic systems, instrumentation, energy, and emerging one as quantum, nano-tech, space-tech, Additive Manufacturing
  • Strong capabilities to drive financial aspects and work closely with CFO office
  • Proven experience in managing and motivating teams of highly skilled technical experts
  • Fluent in English and French

Interpersonal skills

  • An inspiring leader with excellent listening skills and the ability to build trust among customers, partners and collaborators
  • Ability to define a vision and to provide clear directions and priorities to execute on it
  • Strong analytical skills combined with structured and critical thinking
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills with industry, academic and institutional stakeholder
  • Proven networking skills, with a strong network in Switzerland, EU and worldwide
  • Open-minded and creative thinking
  • Able to work under pressure
  • Customer- and service-oriented mindset and solution-driven approach
