Full description

An institution under cantonal public law, the CNP's mission is to conduct the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The CNP aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community-based psychiatric model; it implements public policies for the promotion and prevention of mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of professional training and research in psychiatry. The Department of Adults is looking for a person to join the outpatient consultations of the Department of Forensic Psychiatry:

Psychologist-psychotherapist 70%

The Forensic Psychiatry Department provides therapeutic support to people subject to an obligation to provide care in the criminal context. Its approach is based on strong values of respect and benevolence, with the ambition of offering individualized care, adapted to the needs and journey of each patient. Care is provided by a multidisciplinary team, including doctors, psychologists and nurses, who bring together views and expertise to respond to complex clinical situations, at the interface between psychiatry and justice.

As part of your job, you will be required to

  • Assess and diagnose patients' behavioral, emotional, and cognitive disorders.
  • To provide psychological and psychotherapeutic follow-ups ordered by criminal law.
  • Carry out clinical evaluations and participate in the development of therapeutic projects.
  • Lead a therapeutic group.
  • Collaborate with the multidisciplinary team and network partners, in particular the enforcement and probation authorities
  • Participate in clinical meetings, supervisions and institutional development times.
  • Ensure the maintenance and administrative follow-up of his/her clinical activity (writing reports, keeping the patient file, entering services)
  • Participate in the supervision and development of assistant psychologists.

The competencies required for this position are as follows

  • Hold a university master's degree in psychology or equivalent recognized by PsyCo.
  • Hold a recognized postgraduate designation.
  • Experience in the prison or penitentiary environment, and being a forensic psychologist are assets.
  • Ability to work with patients with complex clinical situations, including in a context of constrained care.
  • Autonomy, ability to collaborate with each other and ability to work in a structured institutional and therapeutic setting.
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary team and collaborate constructively.
  • To know the organisation of the Swiss and cantonal health system.

We offer you

  • A key role at the heart of the functioning of clinical activities.
  • A mission with a strong societal impact, at the crossroads of mental health, justice and public safety issues.
  • A rich and stimulating clinical practice, with patients with complex psychiatric problems.
  • Time for clinical reflection, exchanges and sharing of expertise, which is essential for the quality of care.
  • Real autonomy in the organization of your work.
  • The opportunity to actively contribute to the evolution of the service and care pathways.
  • A multidisciplinary work environment, in close collaboration with the medical, nursing and administrative teams.
  • The opportunity to develop your professional skills within a recognised cantonal institution.
  • Working conditions governed by the CLA Santé 21 and attractive social benefits.

Start date: November 1, 2026 or to be agreed Place of work: CPCM, La Chaux-de-Fonds and CPCL, Marin-Epagnier Activity rate: 70% Type of contract: Permanent contract Contact person: Jérémy Terrat, Chief Medical Officer (Forensic Psychiatry Department) Complete application documents (cover letter, CV, diplomas and work certificates) must be sent by direct online application.
