Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad at La Chaux-de-Fonds as a Technician.ne Quality Assembly Movement (f/m/x) and be part of this extraordinary mission! We are currently looking for our next colleague to complete our #Squad! You will be attached to the site of La Chaux-de-Fonds. It is more than just a position of Technician.ne Quality Assembly, it is a position in which you can put all your knowledge in order to participate actively in the improvement and development of the activity.

Your contribution

  • You make the visiting sheets and quality documents.
  • You ensure quality support and support production teams in their quality approaches.
  • You ensure the monitoring of preseries as well as the certification of T1 components.
  • You record, follow and deal with non-compliance.
  • You identify and raise issues, while being the power of proposal to implement adapted solutions.
  • You ensure compliance with quality standards, including through post audits.
  • You are involved in continuous improvement projects.
  • You analyze production waste, monitor it and initiate corrective and curative actions.
  • You make decisions within the framework of the quality exemption stream, in accordance with the procedures in force.
  • You develop and follow the quality performance indicators (KPI) of the workshops.
  • You ensure the relationship and quality monitoring with external subcontractors.
  • You communicate in an appropriate, synthetic and structured way with all your contacts.
  • You write clear, accurate and quality reports. Do you think there's much more to discover in this position? You're right! Apply today and learn more. You will integrate perfectly with our team if:
  • You have a diploma in ES Quality Technician or an equivalent qualification, supplemented by a course in watchmaking. A TQ1 and/or TQ2 certification will be an asset.
  • You justify 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar function, ideally in the watchmaking field, with expertise in movements.
  • You have strong knowledge of watch components, especially movements.
  • You master problem solving tools, reading plans and ISO rating, as well as general metrology principles.
  • You can easily use common IT tools, including Microsoft Excel. Knowledge of Quick-Control software will be an asset.
  • You have an excellent spirit of analysis and logic. Knowledge in Statistical Process Control (MSP) will be a plus.
  • You are autonomous, methodical, rigorous and proactive in your work.
  • You are recognized for your communication skills and ability to write clear and structured documents.
  • You are a strong proponent and actively contribute to continuous improvement.
  • You have perfect command of French, both oral and written. Knowledge in English and/or German will be an asset. We invite you to apply even if you do not meet all of these criteria.
