Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Do you like technical challenges, good work and colleagues on whom to rely? Our Boudry branch is looking for new talent to strengthen its team! You will be able to participate in major projects, with various projects, mainly in the field of industry. At Groupe E, you will join a team that is united, where mutual support, trust and a good atmosphere are part of everyday life. You will have a modern and enjoyable working environment to value your experience, meet new challenges and continue to develop your skills.

Installer or installer CFC 80-100%

Mission

  • Perform various electrical installations, mainly on large-scale construction sites and in the industrial field
  • Install, connect and modify electrical equipment: tables, apparatus, communication systems, multimedia, security and control
  • Ensure the reading and interpretation of electrical plans and diagrams, as well as the laying of pipes and the drawing of cables
  • Participate in commissioning and ensure the quality, compliance and safety of facilities
  • Establish the metres and governance reports required to monitor interventions
  • Share your know-how and contribute to the training of our apprentices

Profile

  • Holder of CFC installer
  • Experience in general electrical installations, ideally acquired on large-scale construction sites
  • Organized and resourceful personality, you show initiative, anticipation and a real sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit and good communication: you enjoy collaborating with your colleagues, sharing your knowledge and contributing to a positive working atmosphere
  • Flexibility and openness, with the desire to meet various technical challenges
  • Good command of French

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance, with four-and-a-half days of working time and free Friday afternoon
  • A function vehicle also suitable for private use
  • Interesting opportunities for change within the company and support in your continuing training
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Neuchâtel's migration office after the contract is signed.
