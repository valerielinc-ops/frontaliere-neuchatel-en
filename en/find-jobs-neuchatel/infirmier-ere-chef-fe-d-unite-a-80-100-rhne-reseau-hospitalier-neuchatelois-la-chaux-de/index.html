Full description

The unit of medicine c and pulmonary rehabilitation is a unit composed of 10 beds of general medicine in acute care and 9 beds of rehabilitation specialized in the care of patients with chronic respiratory disease.

Nurse Head of Unit at 80-100%

Your missions

  • Lead and mentor a team of about 30 employees
  • Manage a unit consisting of beds dedicated to lung rehabilitation and medicine with the specificities of each discipline
  • Ensure efficient patient care and quality of care consistent with institutional care values
  • Organize unity optimally in collaboration with the various internal and external partners

Your profile

  • Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing or equivalent title recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Authorization to practise a health profession in the canton of Neuchâtel
  • Management certificate or title deemed equivalent, plus
  • Team coaching experience, an asset
  • Minimum 3 years work experience in acute care unit
  • Knowledge of different benefits related to pulmonary rehabilitation and medicine
  • Interest and Team Management Skills
  • Knowledge of the cantonal health network

Your skills

  • Ability to lead, manage and unite a team
  • A sense of responsibility and natural leadership
  • Relationship skills in communication and conflict management
  • Planning and organizational capacity
  • Teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Direction of priorities, adaptation and ability to manage the unexpected
  • Resistance to pressure and stress
  • Professional commitment, flexibility and adaptability
