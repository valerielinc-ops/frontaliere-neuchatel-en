Full description

Our multi-site hospital for physical care, oriented to the quality of care and personalized care of patients, offers you the opportunity to give a new boost to your career by marking your footprint in the department of geriatrics, rehabilitation and palliative care of HRNe.

Deputy Chief Medical Officer of the 80-100% Geriatric Rehabilitation Scheme (possibility of job sharing)

Our offer

  • A dynamic working environment within a team of experienced and recognised professionals
  • An environment for developing innovative projects in the field of stationary, semi-stationary and outpatient rehabilitation
  • The possibility of carrying out clinical research in collaboration with different university hospitals or other reference centres
  • Attractive framework and working conditions governed by the CLC of the executive doctors of the Neuchâtel hospital network
  • Multi-site workplace
  • A function between 80 and 100% with possibility of job sharing
  • Entry into service from 1 December 2026 or to be agreed

Your missions

  • Management and development of the RHN Geriatric Rehabilitation and Internal Medicine Program
  • Supervision and training of assistant doctors, head of clinic in an environment recognised by the ISFM for the training of specialist in geriatrics
  • Close collaboration in the areas of rehabilitation with different upstream specialties (neurology, medicine, geriatrics, rheumatology and orthopaedics) as well as with the extra-cantonal reference hospitals

Your profile

  • Federal diploma of doctor or equivalent foreign title with Mebeko recognition and titles in the Register of Medical Professions (MedReg)
  • Title of specialist in internal medicine (FMH or equivalent) with f.a. of Geriatrics
  • Confirmed experience in project management and management
  • Multidisciplinary leadership training, an asset
  • Excellent knowledge of the French language (level C1 required)

Your skills

  • A sense of responsibility, organization and initiative
  • Professional commitment, flexibility and good adaptability
  • Major ability to work in teams and networks, interscient
  • Leadership, proven management skills and availability
  • Autonomy, decision-making and implementation capacity

Full applications (motivation letter, Curriculum Vitae, diplomas, work certificates) are due until 30 September 2026.

The Neuchâtel hospital network (RHNe) includes the sites of Pourtalès (Neuchâtel), La Chaux-de-Fonds, Val-de-Travers, Val-de-Ruz, Le Locle and La Chrysalide. More than 2,600 collaborators provide acute care, rehabilitation and palliative care for the Neuchâtel population.
