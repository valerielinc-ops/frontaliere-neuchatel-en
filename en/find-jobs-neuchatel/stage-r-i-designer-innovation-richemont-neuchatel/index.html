Full description

Reference code: JR136371

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

As early as March 2027 and for a period of 6 months, we are looking for a creative and curious trainee to help our teams stimulate their creativity and materialize their ideas within our department of Research and Innovation.

LIABILITY

In direct collaboration with the Transversal Innovation Office team and under the responsibility of your internship manager, you will have the role of accompanying teams through the application of design methods to foster innovation. Specifically, you will be required to:

  • Participate in creativity workshops with internal teams (you will be trained and guided on these aspects).
  • Support innovation projects with a user-centred design approach (user research, design, prototyping and testing)
  • Support teams in creating 2D and 3D renderings
  • Explore and formalize innovative concepts through visual media (studies, storyboards, UX/UI models, videos)

PROFILE

You are a student in the final year of master's degree in design or with a specialization in design (industrial, product, innovation, UX, ..). Hybrid profiles are also appreciated.

You have an UX/IU sensitivity and interest in interaction design, ergonomics and user experience.

You also have an interest in ideas methods and the ability to work collaboratively. You have an interest in AI tools.

You master the Adobe suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro), Procreate and ideally Figma.

Creative.ve, dynamic and organized.e, you are the force of proposal. The mastery of English is a plus.

Based mainly in Microcity, Neuchâtel, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research areas within our department.

We look forward to receiving your application (CV, Motivation Letter and Portfolio)!
