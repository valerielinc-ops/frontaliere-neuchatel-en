Full description

Reference code: JR134154

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

From February 2027 we are looking for a 6-month intern for the conduct of work on data collection and improvement of a chemical process for watchmaking decoration.

LIABILITY

In direct collaboration with the Materials, Chemistry and Surfaces team, under the responsibility of your internship tutor, your missions will be as follows:

  • Conducting bibliographical research;
  • Status, design and implementation of data collection;
  • Data analysis and prediction of drifts and failures;
  • Based on the progress of the previous steps, use of the collected data to improve the prediction of the process;
  • Reporting of work in the form of technical notes and regular presentations.

PROFILE

  • You are a master's or equivalent student and have solid knowledge in data analysis, statistics and machine learning techniques.
  • Able to carry out an experimental campaign and analyse its results, you have a special interest in watchmaking and innovation.
  • Dynamic, organized and autonomous, you have a good spirit of synthesis and are the force of proposition. You're a man of editorial convenience and analytical rigor.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience within a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in La Chaux-de-Fonds in the premises of the Manufacture ProCadrans, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.
