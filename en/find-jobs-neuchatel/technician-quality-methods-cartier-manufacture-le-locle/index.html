CONTEXT

Within the Quality department of our Joaillere Manufacture located in the Locle, we are looking for a quality technician to ensure the reliability of dimensional controls and to support the industrialisation of new products, providing a strong expertise in 3D metrology, control methods and dimensional analysis.

WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

You guarantee the reliability of dimensional controls and support the industrialization of new products, providing a strong expertise in 3D metrology, control methods and dimensional analysis.

YOUR MISSION

DEVELOPING AND IMPROVING 3D and Optical Controls

  • You develop and optimize Zeiss / Calypso and Keyence measurement programs.
  • You design and validate the pods to ensure reliable and reproducible measurements.
  • You improve control methods to reduce variability and measurement times.
  • You ensure the management and traceability of control programs.

ACCOMPANY NEW PRODUCTS

  • You participate in plan reviews and bring your expertise in ISO-GPS rating and measurement.
  • You define control strategies and ranges tailored to product/process risks.
  • You qualify the new means and methods of control: MSA, Gage R&R, Cp/Cpk.
  • You set up the monitoring plans and accompany their deployment in production.
  • You help create flaws and validate test processes.

BEING THE REFERENCE METROLOGY

  • You bring your expertise to dimensional issues and non-compliance.
  • You analyze the measurement results and propose improvements to the Methods and Production teams.
  • You change the ranges and means of control according to field needs.
  • You guarantee the reliability and availability of measurement means: calibration, maintenance and monitoring.

PROGRESS CONTROL PRACTICES

  • You set and improve workshop control standards.
  • You train and accompany operators, controllers and regulators.
  • You audit control practices and identify directions for progress.
  • You will monitor new measurement technologies and participate in the choice of future equipment.

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

You have a Bac+2/Bac+3 in physics or technical training in metrology, micromechanics or an equivalent field. You have strong metrology and quality skills, as well as a good knowledge of the precision industry. You master ISO-GPS standards, reading complex plans, dimensional metrology and assessing measurement uncertainties. You can conduct MSA and Gage R&R studies, analyze process capability using Cp and Cpk indicators, and develop monitoring plans and control cards.

You have advanced control of Zeiss equipment and Calypso software. You program the optical measurement means Keyence and design control positions with CREO. You also use Ellistat and master Microsoft 365 tools.

Your knowledge of precision machining and precious materials allows you to understand the factors that influence the reliability of measurements.
