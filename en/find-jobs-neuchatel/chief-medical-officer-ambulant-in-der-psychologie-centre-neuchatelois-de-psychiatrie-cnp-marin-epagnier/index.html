Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The outpatient area of the department of advanced age (CNPâa) offers a comprehensive care offer in the Canton of Neuchâtel. Our multidisciplinary teams accompany patients aged 65 and over with complex psychiatric disorders in an integrative and person-centred approach. As chief physician of the ambulance in advanced psychiatry, you will be responsible for the clinical and organizational conduct of an area with extensive missions: Consultations, day hospital, mobile team, and intra-hospital liaison.
