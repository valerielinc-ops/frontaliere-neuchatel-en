Full description

In order to strengthen our teams in the canton of Neuchâtel, we are looking for a 100% H/F hydraulic heater. Hydraulic/heater mounting Your missions

  • Provide TIG shaping and welding of black/inox steel networks;
  • Mastering the techniques of piping and crimping of heating and air conditioning networks;
  • Mastering the installation of climate equipment and the installation of supporting equipment;
  • Ensure on-site verification and receipt of purchased products;
  • Report the work of the site to the business manager or construction manager;
  • Ensure the relationship with other state bodies on the site;
  • Monitoring the conformity of the work;
  • Address and monitor non-conformities with respect to its construction site
  • Occasional management of ventilation facilities.

What characterises you

  • CFC installer in heating or diploma deemed equivalent;
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in an equivalent position;
  • Capable of reading plans, schematics and scoring;
  • Proactivity, concern for safety rules, ordered

Here's what we offer you

  • Good social benefits
