Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Care Directorate is looking for its hospital nursing pool, one of which is:

Nurse in hospital pool 50%

You have a HES nursing degree or a qualification deemed equivalent and recognised by the Swiss Red Cross. You want to join a recognized institution specializing in mental health. As a nurse in our hospital pool, you will need to be adaptable and flexible because you will work in our hospital units in our three adult, advanced and adolescent departments (as needed).

As part of your function you will be required to

  • identify patient needs and resources at the beginning of the care intervention and develop care objectives and actions taking into account a comprehensive therapeutic project
  • managing acute and complex clinical situations with professionalism and empathy
  • receiving and accompanying people in situations of emotional and/or social crisis
  • ensuring accurate and complete documentation of care interventions

The competencies required for this position are

  • HES nursing degree or qualification deemed equivalent or recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • having several years of experience in psychiatry would be an asset
  • ability to work flexibly and adapt to different departments / type of schedules
  • Demonstrate a spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • mastery of the French language in writing and oral
