Full description

An establishment under cantonal public law, the CNP has the mission of leading the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The CNP aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders through the adoption of an integrated and community psychiatry model; it implements public policies for the promotion and prevention of mental health; it acts as a reference center in the field of professional training and research in psychiatry. The Care Department is looking for several: Outpatient nurses 80 - 100% As part of an interdisciplinary team, you assess the needs of patients in order to co-construct an individualized recovery project. You provide therapeutic care in collaboration with the network and those around you. Your practice, oriented towards recovery in psychiatry, aims to support the skills and autonomy of patients. As part of your role you will be required to:

  • Work in collaboration with an interdisciplinary team made up in particular of doctors, psychologists, social workers,
  • Carry out an assessment of the person's resources and needs to develop an individualized recovery project,
  • Carry out treatment with a therapeutic aim in the field of psychoeducation, in partnership with those close to them and partners in the care network,
  • Provide relational care oriented towards the recovery of patients in psychiatry and mental health,
  • Support patients in developing new coping strategies and restoring individual skills with a view to reintegrating into the community,
  • Facilitate therapeutic groups. The skills required for these positions are as follows:
  • Have an HES diploma in nursing or a title deemed equivalent and recognized by the Swiss Red Cross,
  • Have at least 3 years of confirmed experience in an acute and outpatient psychiatric care service,
  • Know the Swiss health system and the Neuchâtel health network,
  • Be mobile and have an excellent capacity for personal organization and adaptation to changes,
  • Work independently and cope with situations of crisis.
