Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP is responsible for conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health. It offers an integrated and community psychiatry, in partnership with local institutions. The NPC is also a reference centre for training and research in psychiatry. The Relational Health Unit (USR) brings together three specialized ambulatory devices: the Couples and Families Consultation (CCF), the Domestic Violence Author Service (SAVC) and the Psychotraumatology Consultation (CPT). The unit offers coordinated care for people facing traumatic experiences, psychological, physical, sexual, economic or social violence, as well as relationship, emotional and family difficulties. The CPT welcomes patients with psychotrauma disorders in an integrative, relational and recovery-oriented approach. Clinical work pays particular attention to the issues of safety, stabilization, therapeutic alliance and continuity of care, in order to support the recovery processes of people facing traumatic experiences. You will join an interdisciplinary team of doctors, psychologists and nurses, invested in a clinic of connection, coordination and networking.

As part of your function, you will be required to

  • Provide nursing support to patients with psychotrauma disorders;
  • Participate in the clinical evaluation and development of the interdisciplinary therapeutic project;
  • Conduct individual, family and network interviews;
  • Support stabilization, emotional regulation and recovery processes;
  • Co-animating verbal and non-verbal therapeutic groups;
  • To work closely with the partners of the social health network to promote continuity of care;
  • Participate in institutional clinical reflection and development of USR services.

The competencies required for this position are

  • HES diploma in nursing or equivalent recognized by the Swiss Red Cross;
  • Confirmed clinical experience in adult psychiatry, ideally in an outpatient setting;
  • Training in psychotraumatology (module 1);
  • Interest in relational, systemic and interdisciplinary approaches;
  • Ability to work in complex situations involving issues of violence and trauma;
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and good collaborative skills.

We offer

  • A specialized and developing clinical environment;
  • An interdisciplinary practice focusing on the quality of the therapeutic link;
  • A team engaged in a psychotrauma clinic focused on the quality of the link and continuity of care;
  • Opportunities for continuing training and clinical skills development.
