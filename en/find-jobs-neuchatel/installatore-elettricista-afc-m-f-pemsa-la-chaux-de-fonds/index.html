Your assignment

  • Run the shooting and laying of cables, channels and technical sheaths
  • Connect electrical paintings and various equipment
  • Read and interpret projects, schematics and technical documents
  • Intervention on new construction sites, renovation and transformation
  • Perform installation jobs in strong current and weak current
  • Respect electrical standards, safety instructions and quality requirements
  • Collaboration with the different operators present in the yards

And now we talk about you?

  • You have achieved a CFC as an electrician
  • You are master of strong current and weak current systems
  • You can read and interpret electrical projects and schemes
  • You are autonomous, rigorous and meticulous in your work
  • You like to work on the team and you can adapt to the different yards
  • You have sense of responsibility and you like well done work
  • Ideally own category B driving license
