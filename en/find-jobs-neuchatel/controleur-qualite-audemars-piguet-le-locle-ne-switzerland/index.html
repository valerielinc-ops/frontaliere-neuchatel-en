Quality Controller — Audemars Piguet

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Within the quality control department, your mission will be to perform the controls of the components at entry.

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Perform aesthetic, dimensional and functional controls of our movement components
  • Monitoring of parts in the ERP
  • Edit computer control reports
  • Make first contact with suppliers in case of non-compliance and on-site controls
  • You have a CFC in micromechanics or watchmaking
  • You benefit from training in the field of quality and justify an experience of at least 5 years in a similar position in the watchmaking field.
  • You master the usual and specific means of measurement (comparator, measuring bench, gauges, durometer, Marcel Aubert profile projector, Microspeed, GR20, friction control device, ...)
  • You have excellent visual acuity and are comfortable with micro and binocular controls.
  • You have good knowledge of MS Office, control software and GPAO. Ellistat's knowledge is a plus.
  • Of a rigorous nature, you have a good analytical spirit and enjoy working as a team

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities
  • Possibility of working remotely from several sites (GE/VD/NE)
  • An LPP with favourable rates
