This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Integrated into Human Resources and under the supervision of the Head of HR, your mission will be to support managers and staff on all HR issues, ensuring an operational advisory role. You will contribute to the organization's performance by driving the life cycle, accompanying transformation, developing talent and supporting teams on a daily basis. In this context, you will be responsible for:

  • Supporting departmental managers in the development of the HR strategy and in all human resources, development and team training issues
  • Acting as a close partner by ensuring a regular HR presence within departments
  • Understand the HR strategy and decline the department's medium/long-term action plan into concrete actions
  • Participate in the definition of the recruitment needs of your target population and carry out the related recruitments (sorting of files, interviews, references)
  • Conduct development, support or reshaping interviews in partnership with managers
  • Monitor and retain talent while acting as a facilitator in change management
  • Participate in various HR projects, in partnership with other entities
  • For the benefit of the Federal Patent of Specialist in Human Resources or higher training in Human Resources, you justify a significant experience in a similar position in the industrial field
  • You have excellent knowledge of French and English both orally and in writing (level B2 of the European standard required)
  • You master HR processes, labour laws and are comfortable with the usual IT tools
  • Organised and autonomous, you have the attention to detail and have a very good sense of service
  • Finally, you have a strong sense of confidentiality, diplomacy and integrity as well as a demonstrated ability to work as a team and influence stakeholders What we offer:
  • Health insurance coverage (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The possibility according to function to work remotely
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL Would you like to express your expertise at Audemars Piguet? Don't hesitate and apply online! We look forward to discussing the opportunities that could match your next professional adventure.
