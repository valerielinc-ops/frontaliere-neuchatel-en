Put to the Point

They independently coordinate decentralized price collection in cooperation with an external contractor. You plan the survey process, take part in the training courses and ensure the quality of the delivered data. You also perform sophisticated price surveys using various computer tools (scanning data, online surveys, energy prices, etc.). They take on tasks in quality assurance and thus contribute to the preparation of the monthly publication of the country index of consumer prices (LIK). In addition, you support the production management in the planning and take on tasks in the event of absences in the team.
