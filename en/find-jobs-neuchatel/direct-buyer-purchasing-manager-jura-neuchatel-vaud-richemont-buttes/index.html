Full description

Reference code: JR136383

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Are you in the purchasing business and want to discover new opportunities in a demanding industrial environment, in the service of the Maisons du Groupe Richemont, in the medium or long term?

In an approach to anticipation of our future needs, we now wish to develop our network of talent in the purchasing professions for our various sites and entities located in the cantons of Jura, Neuchâtel & Vaud.

HOW CAN YOU HAVE AN IMPACT WITH US?

Depending on future opportunities, your expertise could contribute to various procurement issues, including:

  • Pilot a supplier portfolio and participate in defining purchasing strategies;
  • Manage consultations, negotiations and contracts with suppliers;
  • Identify and support partners meeting quality, cost, timeliness and performance requirements;
  • Participate in sourcing, risk reduction and security of supply;
  • Monitor supplier performance and contribute to their development;
  • Work closely with Supply Chain, Production, Quality, R&D, Finance and Trade teams;
  • Contribute to continuous improvement projects and responsible procurement;
  • For managers profiles, define purchasing priorities, support teams and manage the performance of your perimeter.

Opportunities may concern different levels of seniority, from buyer to Purchasing Manager.

WHAT PROFILES ARE WE WANT TO KNOW?

In particular, we would like to exchange with professionals who have:

  • Advanced training in Purchasing, Supply Chain, Engineering, Economics or equivalent;
  • Experience in purchasing in an industrial environment;
  • A good mastery of sourcing processes, negotiation and management of the supplier relationship;
  • A capacity to work with multiple interlocutors and in demanding environments;
  • A spirit of analysis, an excellent sense of communication and a genuine negotiating capacity;
  • A good mastery of French and English.

Depending on future needs, we may need to look for expertise in direct purchases, components, materials, equipment or services related to our industrial activities.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as in our ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value empathy, humility and integrity.
  • We encourage collegiality, curiosity and courage.
  • We take care of the world we live in.

YOUR CONTACT WITH US

By joining this pool, you allow us to better understand your career path, your expertise and your professional aspirations.

This is not an application for a currently vacant position and this does not guarantee that an opportunity will be offered.

If, in the future, a need corresponding to your profile would arise within one of our entities in the cantons of Jura, Neuchâtel & Vaud, our Talent Acquisition team could resume contact with you to discuss this opportunity.

Want to stay in touch with us for future opportunities in Purchasing? We'll be happy to discover your profile.
