Full description

The manufacturing sector of the HR pharmacy serviceNever begin a significant technical and organizational transformation phase. In this context of development, our department wishes to strengthen its production team.

Your missions

  • Supporting the pharmacist responsible for conducting the manufacturing sector
  • Supervising routine manufacturing activities: cytostatic, aseptic and non-sterile magistral preparations
  • Participate in the monitoring of infrastructure and equipment constituting the technical environment of the sector
  • Participate in the maintenance and development of documentation and information systems related to the manufacturing sector

Your profile

  • Federal diploma in pharmacists or equivalent by MEBEKO
  • Authorization to practice independently
  • Title FPH in Hospital Pharmacy or deemed equivalent
  • Experience in pharmaceutical manufacturing and knowledge of GMP
  • Insulator production experience and knowledge of the desired CATO software
  • Good control of common computer tools

Your skills

  • Enjoy working as a team in a healthy collaborative environment based on trust and knowledge sharing
  • You have a developed mind for the organization and management of the unexpected
  • You have a sense of responsibility and autonomy that allows you to take good initiatives
  • You communicate with ease and benevolence and appreciate interdisciplinary collaboration
