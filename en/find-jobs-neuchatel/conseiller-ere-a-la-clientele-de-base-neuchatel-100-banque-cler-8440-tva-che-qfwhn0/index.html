Core Customer Advisor Neuchâtel 100%

As a young subsidiary of Basler Kantonalbank, we launched "Zak", the first smartphone bank on the Swiss market. So we're a faster, smarter, and maybe even tougher than the other banks. We are also addressing the issue of money openly and, in general, we are talking about issues that matter directly to us. For example, the need for more women to enter positions of responsibility. That is why we particularly welcome women's applications. What about men? They are of course always welcome. Would you like to learn more about us, who we are and how we function? With pleasure.

Julien, Brad, Sylvain and other colleagues need backup. In other words, they need you!

Your mission

  • Provide comprehensive and needs-based client advice in the areas of basic services, such as Payment Traffic, Savings, Investments and Planning.
  • Proactively manage and develop the client portfolio
  • Transfer customers to other services after identifying their potential (individual and private banking clients)
  • Enthusiasm customers for our excellent products and services.
  • Participate in the organization and accompany customer events with style and elegance

Your contribution

  • Friendly contact with others.
  • The pleasure of selling.
  • The ability to get customers excited.
  • Commercial training in the financial sector or in a similar field.
  • Two years minimum experience in the banking sector.
  • Curiosity and openness to new technologies and digitisation.
  • Good knowledge of German an asset.

Your Benefits

  • The question of money addressed openly, including about equal pay – which is very real in us
  • Exciting opportunities for development – we are at your side to support and advise you.
  • A work within a simple team, involved and endowed with an excellent mind.
  • A workplace in the city centre, restyled and modern.
  • You expect us to have a full job package? You're right. Here you can discover the details and many other benefits.

P.S.: Because we're looking for a bank professional and not an advertising writer, you can spare the trouble of writing a cover letter. Instead, we would prefer you to answer three small questions in the application tool. Thank you!
