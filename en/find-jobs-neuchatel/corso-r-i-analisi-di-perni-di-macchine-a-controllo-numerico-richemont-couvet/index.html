Full description

Reference code: JR134118

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

We are looking for 6 months, a trainee to carry out study work for the optimization of numerically controlled machine tools in our manufacturing research and innovation laboratory.

LIABILITY

The study will focus on the analysis of pins of numerically controlled machines to optimize the overall performance of the machining process, through data collection, post-processing and implementation of solutions on production machines.

More specifically, you will be responsible for

  • Perform a state of art on existing technologies;
  • Implementation of behaviour observation routines for NMCAs;
  • Spindle tests and vibratory analyses;
  • To be a force for proposals on technological means to be put in place;
  • To explore and identify specialized external partners and suppliers;
  • Synthesize technological/performance results;
  • Skills required: mechanical, machining, signal processing and programming.

PROFILE

  • Engineering student, engineer/master level specialized in mechanics, machining, data acquisition and processing;
  • Skills and/or experience in material removal processes and cutting mechanisms;
  • Interest in practical machine testing combined with a rigorous scientific approach;
  • Persevering and autonomous;
  • Curious.se, creative.ve, autonomous and powerful proposal;
  • Taste for teamwork and the desire to learn.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience within a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in Couvet, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!
