Full description

For Banque Raiffeisen des Montagnes Neuchâteloises, we are looking for a person as a Middle Office Collaborator.

Add

  • Support for Middle Office administrative activities with a high level of precision and reliability,
  • Management and monitoring of customer files related to banking products, real estate financing as well as investment solutions,
  • Close support to advisors in the preparation and monitoring of their activities, particularly in terms of credits and investments,
  • Participation in the processing and control of investment operations in compliance with regulatory processes and requirements,
  • Analysis of files, proactive identification of blocking points and proposal of concrete solutions,
  • Regular contact with customers: monitoring of requests, reminders and coordination with professionalism,
  • Active contribution to the quality of files, compliance with compliance and the security of operations,
  • Close collaboration with internal teams in a spirit of mutual support and collective efficiency.

Anforderungen

  • Banking training completed and strong interest in a central role in Middle Office,
  • Recognized rigor, sense of organization and reliability,
  • Ability to handle varied files methodically, while maintaining a good overview,
  • Analytical spirit and pragmatic, solution-oriented approach,
  • Relational ease and sense of service, with a natural approach to customer contact,
  • Autonomy, sense of responsibility and ability to take relevant initiatives,
  • Team spirit and desire to contribute to a collaborative dynamic,
  • Flexibility and ability to adapt in a changing environment,
  • Good command of IT tools and comfort in a digital environment.

Please note

Cooperative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape the way we collaborate and behave towards each other. The culture of familiarity promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all job levels.
