Seller is a caterer

Coop Genossenschaft — Neuchâtel, NE, Svizzera

Mission

  • You make homemade products (cut cheese, sandwiches, etc.) according to the current concepts

  • You are in charge of customer orders and their preparation throughout the year
  • You strictly follow our guidelines (hygiene, temperature instructions, expiry dates, etc.)

Qualifications

  • Open and dynamic, you appreciate teamwork
  • Organized, you demonstrate versatility, autonomy and initiative in your work

  • Discounts and discounts: 5 times more superpoints in supermarkets, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop group, mobile and prepaid subscriptions at a reduced price at Coop Mobile, discount to staff also granted to the partner (living under the same roof)
  • Conditions of employment: pension fund: taken over by Coop of 2/3 of contributions, attractive conditions of employment and guaranteed equal pay, collective labour agreement (CCT), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, seniority premiums
  • Working time: 5 weeks of vacation
  • Continuing training: Over 600 in-house training courses, support for external training and development courses, Talent management with many and various development opportunities
  • Sustainable development: A commitment to man and animals and nature with more than 400 acts
  • Communication: internal app dedicated to staff
  • Health: sports activities: volleyball tournaments and hikes
