CONTEXT

In our jewellery factory, we are looking for an Operational Excellence Internship to conduct process and performance optimization missions. The Department of Operational Excellence guarantees continuous improvement for all departments, from production workshops to support functions (Logistics, Quality, Development, Finance...)

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT?

As an Operational Excellence Intern, your main responsibilities will be:

Leading a mission of Operational Excellence within the Swiss Jewellery Centre •

Framework: planning, governance bodies, objectives, deliverables

Conducting the mission according to the DMAIC approach

Coordinate the progress of the project with the various actors

Ensure regular reporting to dedicated bodies

Effective communication and leadership of change

Ensure deliverables and results are achieved

Support departments in their progress efforts

Conduct improvement projects to support departments (5S, visual management, work standards, tool improvement)

Support or train trades in operational excellence methodologies (VSM, DMAIC, Kaizen)

Contribute to the culture of Operational Excellence within the Swiss Jewellery Centre

Communicating and valuing field actions

Capitalizing on Lean's good practices, proposing new initiatives or methods of Operational Excellence for Manufacturing

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

Having a degree in engineering (or equivalent), you justify first experience in a similar position, ideally acquired in an industrial environment.

You have a good knowledge of concepts, tools and methodologies of Operational Excellence and Lean Manufacturing (VSM, DMAIC, Kaizen, 8D etc.). You are pragmatic and know how to listen to the needs of business teams.

You're curious, organized and rigorous. You like to work independently and proactively. You master the basics of project management (planning, reporting, animation of workshops).

Your communication skills, team spirit and good relationship allow you to work effectively with a variety of interlocutors, to foster commitment to change.

WHAT HAVE WE OFFERED TO YOU?

We create an innovative and positive environment where we are proud to employ talents from different backgrounds, experiences and identities. We are convinced that when diversity and inclusion are fully accepted and strengthened, creativity and knowledge emerge to achieve excellence.

YOUR EXPERIENCE WITH US

We invite you to submit your complete application directly to our careers website. If your profile meets our expectations, you will receive an invitation for a delayed video interview. It will be an opportunity to introduce yourself, express your motivations and share your expectations.

Our HR team will then analyze your interview. If your application is successful, you will receive, a few days before October 1, 2026, an invitation for our Talents Day, our 100% digital recruitment day. This immersive day in the heart of the Cartier universe will allow you to meet a HR reference and a manager. You will participate in a group case study, followed by an individual interview.
