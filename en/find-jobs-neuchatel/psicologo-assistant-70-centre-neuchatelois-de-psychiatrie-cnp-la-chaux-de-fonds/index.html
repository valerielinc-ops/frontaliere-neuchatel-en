Full description

The CNP recruits a 70% assistant psychologist to support mental health.

Tasks

  • Provide psychotherapeutic care under supervision.
  • Collaborate in the development of the patient therapeutic project.
  • Participate in multidisciplinary meetings for complex cases.

Competences

  • Master in Psychology or recognized equivalent title.
  • Knowledge of the Swiss and cantonal health system.
  • Prison experience is an asset.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The Department of Adult Research, to join the Legal Psychiatry Service one·e:

Psychologist-Assistant 70%

The Legal Psychiatry Service consists of the Department of Medicine and Penitentiary Psychiatry (SMPP) and Transition Consultation. The activity is separated between the canton's prisons and the outpatient consultation sites of the CNP. We provide therapeutic support to persons incarcerated and/or subject to a duty of care in the criminal context. The psychologist-assistant activity takes place in the SMPP of the Chaux de Fonds (Prison La Promenade) at 40% and at the CPCM at 30%. The approach is based on strong values of respect and benevolence, with the ambition to offer individualised care adapted to the needs and journeys of each patient. Care is provided by a multidisciplinary team, including doctors, psychologists and nurses, who cross-examines and expertise to respond to complex clinical situations.

As part of your duties, you will be required to

  • Provide, on medical prescription, psychotherapeutic care under the supervision of the senior psychologist/psychotherapist or the responsible psychologist.
  • Conduct therapeutic assessments and indications from a clinical evaluation.
  • To collaborate in the development, implementation and updating of the patient therapy project.
  • Perform psychotherapeutic treatments.
  • Maintain patient record and participate in reporting.
  • Ensure the administrative monitoring of its clinical activity, seizure of invoicing services.
  • Collaborate with the multidisciplinary network including the judicial authorities and the prison administration.
  • Participate in multidisciplinarity reflection and clinical development times.
  • Contribute to meetings on the definition of departmental projects and guidelines.

The competencies required for this position are

  • To hold a master's degree in Psychology or equivalent recognized at the federal level,
  • Know the organisation of the Swiss and cantonal health system,
  • Mastering the French language and the usual computer tools,
  • Be comfortable working independently and on a team.
  • Having experience in the prison or prison environment, and being a forensic psychologist are assets.

We offer you

  • A mission with high societal impact, at the crossroads of mental health, justice and public safety issues.
  • A rich and stimulating clinical practice with patients with complex psychiatric problems.
  • Time for clinical reflection, exchanges and sharing of expertise, essential to the quality of management.
  • A multidisciplinary working environment, in close collaboration with medical, health and administrative teams.
  • The possibility of developing your professional skills within a recognised cantonal institution.
  • Working conditions governed by the CLC Health 21 and attractive social benefits.
