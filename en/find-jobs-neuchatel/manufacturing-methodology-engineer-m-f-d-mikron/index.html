Full description

As a Manufacturing Methodology Engineer, you ensure a central role between project teams, assembly and supply chain. Thanks to your technical expertise, you help optimize costs, manage delays and ensure the smooth running of projects to ensure the satisfaction of our internal clients (project teams) and our end customers. Key responsibilities

  • Analyze the actual needs of projects and identify the most appropriate technical solutions, taking into account previous experiences and achievements
  • Ensure the feasibility of the parts to be manufactured (processes, materials, treatments, tolerances), in accordance with quality, cost and time requirements
  • Provide Supply Chain expertise to project teams from the design stage to anticipate risks related to quality, cost and timeliness
  • Acting as technical reference for project teams on issues related to manufacturing processes and Supply Chain
  • Coordinate project milestones with Supply Chain teams
  • Manage nomenclatures and requirements in the ERF
  • Define the most suitable manufacturing processes according to the type of parts
  • Contribute to internal manufacturing or subcontracting decisions (Make or Buy)
  • Develop the operating ranges
  • Monitoring critical parts budgets
  • Follow Supply Chain priorities and contribute to the resolution of blocking points in collaboration with relevant teams
  • Providing technical support in dealing with non-compliance
  • Provide technical expertise on internal and external manufacturing processes
  • Ensure technological monitoring to identify new solutions and optimization opportunities
  • Participate in continuous improvement of Supply Chain processes

Key relational skills

  • Effective communication
  • Team spirit and collaboration
  • Self-control
  • Solutions orientation
  • Adaptability

Technical expertise

  • Rigor and accuracy
  • Autonomy
  • Time and priority management
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks
  • Analysis and problem solving

Profile

  • Initial technical CFC training in mechanics or electrotechnics (or equivalent training)
  • Advanced further training (Bachelor HES, ES Technician, Production Expert Patent or Federal Process Officer Patent)
  • Very good knowledge in mechanics, machining and/or electrotechnical, with at least 3 years experience in a similar industry
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written, and very good knowledge of English (level B2). German is an asset
  • Good control of Microsoft Office tools, especially Excel
  • Experience in using ERP and PLM systems. Knowledge of SAP, Inventor or Vault is an advantage

What We Offer

  • A motivating and stimulating position, with development prospects within a company that is responsive to the continuing training needs of its employees, quality and customer satisfaction
  • A multicultural, collegial, dynamic environment focused on teamwork and continuous improvement
  • The opportunity to meet many challenges in a technical, industrial and supply chain environment
  • An open-minded and humane business culture
