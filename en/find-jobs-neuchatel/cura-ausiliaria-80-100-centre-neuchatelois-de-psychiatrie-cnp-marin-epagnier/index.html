Full description

The CNP is looking for an auxiliary carer in psychiatry. Join a team committed to patient welfare.

Tasks

  • Care for elderly people with mental disorders.
  • Assist patients in daily body care.
  • Participate in the maintenance of care facilities and equipment.

Competences

  • Holder of an AFP in Red Cross care or certificate.
  • psychiatry experience appreciated.
  • Good relational sense and adaptation to care.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The department of advanced age is seeking, for its hospital unit G1, one:

Auxiliary caregiver 80-100%

You have an AFP for Care and Accompaniment (ASA) or a health assistant certificate from the Swiss Red Cross, or a title deemed equivalent, and you wish to join a recognized institution specializing in mental health.

As part of your function you will be required to

  • Care for persons of advanced age who are mentally ill, taking into account their habits and needs, and promoting their autonomy and independence.
  • Helping patients achieve daily body care: grooming, dressing, feeding, mobilizing, communicating.
  • Participate in the maintenance of patient care equipment, service facilities and the patient environment.
  • Ensure that patient social ties are maintained.

The core competencies required are

  • Holder of an AFP for Care and Accompaniment (ASA) or a health certificate from the Swiss Red Cross, or equivalent.
  • Have specific experience and knowledge in psychiatry (an asset).
  • Demonstrate a common sense of relationship.
  • Have a capacity to adapt to different care situations (acute and stabilized).
