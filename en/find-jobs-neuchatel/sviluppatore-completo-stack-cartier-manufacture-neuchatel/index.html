Contract with maximum duration of 12 months

Background

Within Cartier's Product Digitalization department, you design, develop and maintain end-to-end web applications. Your goal is to provide business teams with intuitive and efficient solutions to meet digital continuity challenges. In close connection with the Product Owners and the systems experts, and on a scope of actions covering the entire Manufacturing of our House, you intervene over the entire application cycle, from the architectural definition to the production.

Your Responsibilities

Front-end development

  • Design modern, ergonomic and responsive web interfaces with React and TypeScript
  • Develop a library of modular and reusable components
  • Ensure display performance and user experience

Back-end development

  • Develop robust application and micro services via Kotlin and Spring Boot
  • Design documented STRP APIs and ensure integration with third-party systems
  • Model, manipulate and optimize relational databases (PostgreSQL)
  • Ensuring the safety, resilience and scalability of services

Architecture and software engineering

  • Participate in the choice of technical architecture and the modernization of the application base
  • Apply code cleanliness standards (clean code, modularity, maintenance)
  • Write automated tests (units and integration) and conduct code reviews

Technical cooperation

  • Analyze functional needs with Product Owners to translate them into technical specifications
  • Actively collaborate with other developers and business partners
  • Be the power of proposal on technologies, tools and continuous improvement of the team
  • Ensure production monitoring, application monitoring and incident resolution

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

You have a degree in engineering or a Master in Computer Science, Software Engineering or equivalent.

You have several years of experience (5 to 8 years) in developing Full-Stack web applications and have a solid mastery of modern technologies and architectures.

Technical expertise requested

  • Front-end: Advanced React Mastery, TypeScript and JavaScript.
  • Back-end: Solid Java or Kotlin, Spring Boot Framework and REST API architectures.
  • Data: Good command of SQL and relational bases (PostgreSQL or equivalent). An interest or experience in Data Visualization is a plus.
  • DevOps: Common use of Git and CI/CD pipelines. Knowledge of Docker/Podman appreciated.

Your personal qualities

  • Autonomy, methodological rigour and problem-solving orientation
  • Strong commitment to quality, sustainability and good code practice
  • Relationship, team and technical curiosity

Workplace: Le Crêt-du-Locle (Neuchâtel, Switzerland)

Telework: 1 day per week (according to the Group guidelines)

WHAT HAVE WE OFFERED TO YOU?

We propose to integrate a team at the heart of the digital transformation of Cartier Manufacture's product development. You will have the opportunity to design high-value applications for business teams and to modernize the Home's tools.

You will evolve in a stimulating technical setting, with real autonomy to guide architecture and technological choices.

Join Cartier and put your Full-Stack expertise at the service of innovation and product excellence. We offer you a modern and dynamic working environment within a prestigious Manufacture that puts humans at the centre in a caring work environment.
