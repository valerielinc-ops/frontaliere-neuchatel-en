Full description

HR Operations Leader (m/f/d) As HR Operations Leader, you guarantee the reliability and efficiency of HR Operations activities for more than 600 employees in Boudry. You combine business expertise, functional leadership and direct operational involvement, especially in payroll production, while ensuring quality, compliance and timeliness. Key responsibilities

  • Ensure the execution of all HR Operations activities: Payroll, Time and Absence Management, Personnel and Contract Administration, HR Data and Systems, Reporting, Compliance and Operational Controls.
  • Act as a reference expert on complex issues related to payroll, social insurance, time management, legal obligations and internal control requirements for HR Operations.
  • As part of the reorganization and digital transformation of HR Operations activities, you identify opportunities for improvement, develop concrete solutions and pilot their implementation and adoption.
  • Combine pilotage and operational execution by directly supporting certain Payroll and HR Operations activities, including payroll entry and processing, while ensuring the resolution of complex cases.
  • Coordinate the activities of the HR Operations team (2 people), promoting autonomy, collaboration and knowledge sharing.
  • Analyze HR Operations data and provide relevant analysis to support Directorate decision-making.
  • Participate in systems and process development projects, particularly around SAP and Workday.
  • Work closely with the HR teams of the site and the Group as well as with all of our key partners internally and externally.

Key relational skills

  • Customer focus and partnership
  • Guidance actions and solutions
  • Accuracy and accuracy
  • Confidentiality and discretion

Technical expertise

  • Autonomy
  • Emotional intelligence
  • Relationship management
  • Time and priority management

Profile

  • Experience of at least 10 years in Payroll and HR Operations, with in-depth expertise in Swiss payroll.
  • Federal social insurance patent or equivalent.
  • Strong experience in HR Operations processes, including personnel administration, HR data and reporting.
  • Proactive, structured, rigorous and solutions oriented personality, with an excellent sense of service and a strong capacity for collaboration.
  • Prior experience in the operational coordination of a small team.
  • Demonstrated ability to combine overall vision and operational involvement.
  • Easy to manage priorities, problematic and complex situations with autonomy and pragmatism.
  • Excellent analytical capabilities and ease in the operation, control and interpretation of HR data.
  • Essential control of SAP; Knowledge of Workday is an advantage.
  • An experience in Payroll International would be an asset.
  • An excellent mastery of French and good oral and written knowledge of English, Italian is an advantage.

We offer you

  • A motivating and stimulating position, with prospects for change within a company that is attentive to the continuing training needs of its employees, to quality and customer satisfaction.
  • A multicultural, collegial, dynamic environment focused on teamwork and continuous improvement.
  • The opportunity to meet many challenges in a technical, industrial and supply chain environment.
  • An open corporate culture of mind and human.
  • The possibility of teleworking one day a week.
