Individual Customer Advisor Neuchâtel 100%

As a young subsidiary of Basler Kantonalbank, we launched "Zak", the first smartphone bank on the Swiss market. So we're a faster, smarter, and maybe even tougher than the other banks. We are also addressing the issue of money openly and, in general, we are talking about issues that matter directly to us. For example, the need for more women to enter positions of responsibility. That is why we particularly welcome women's applications. What about men? They are of course always welcome. Would you like to learn more about us, who we are and how we function? With pleasure.

David, Elisabete, Karim, Nathan, Melissa and other colleagues need backup. In other words, they need you!

Your mission

  • Provide comprehensive and needs-based client advice in the areas of Finance, Investments and Planning.
  • Manage and proactively develop a portfolio of clients.
  • Enthusiasm customers for our excellent products and services.
  • Create networks and contribute to the reputation of our bank in your city (representation tasks).
  • Participate in the organization and accompany customer events with style and elegance.

Your contribution

  • 3-5 years of professional experience at least as an advisor with management of your own client portfolio.
  • Excellent knowledge of mortgages and investments.
  • User-friendly contact with others and the ability to energize customers.
  • The pleasure of selling, especially in the field of investments.
  • SAQ certification of individual customer advisor desired.
  • A solution-oriented thinking capacity.

Your Benefits

  • The question of money addressed openly, including about equal pay – which is very real in us
  • A positive and peaceful team spirit
  • A workstation downtown.
  • You expect us to have a full job package? You're right. Here you can discover the details and many other benefits.

Any questions?
