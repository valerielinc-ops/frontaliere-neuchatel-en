Full description

Reference code: JR135730

Found in Florence in 1860, Panerai forged its reputation by developing precision instruments and professional watches for the Royal Italian Navy, renewed for their reliability and functional design. Rooted in performance, purpose, and precision, the Maison embraces its heritage while pushing the boundaries of innovation, combining Italian design with Swiss expertise. Today, Panerai continues to create tool watches that serve as trusted companions for every adventure, even in the most demanding environment.

As a future Master Data Project Manager, you will play a key role in our ambition to strengthen the management and optimization of the Master Data.

Guaranteeing the quality, consistency and reliability of data through our systems, you will contribute to their governance and evolution in a multicultural, complex and stimulating ecosystem.

Your Impact

  • Lead and coordinate Master Data & Compliance projects: you will lead initiatives to strengthen our data management and compliance of our processes.
  • As a Product Owner, you will be responsible for our Product Certification Flow Management (PEGA) tool. You will define requirements, prioritize developments, coordinate developments and maximize the value given to the company.
  • Orchestrate Data Governance initiatives related to data quality, ensuring the adoption of the Monte Carlo platform, the development of Data Quality monitors and the exploitation of data in Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • Working with transversal and international teams: you will work closely with many stakeholders to ensure the harmonisation of data and compliance standards across different markets.
  • Identify areas for continuous improvement and propose innovative solutions to enhance data reliability
  • Ensure the maintenance, quality and sustainability of documentation on deployed solutions, in the context of projects and product management.

Your profile

You are a project management professional at the interface between business, data and systems issues, with experience in a complex and international environment, combining:

  • A degree in engineering or in a related field, supplemented by at least 3 years of experience in project management, ideally in the field of Master Data and/or compliance.
  • A mastery of SQL and familiarity with Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
  • SAP knowledge and ease with Atlassian tools, including JIRA and Confluence.
  • A good command of English and an ability to evolve with ease in intercultural contexts.
  • A structured and rigorous approach, allowing you to plan, estimate workloads and track developments, while effectively translating business needs into technical specifications.
  • Autonomy, sense of priorities and excellent relational qualities to coordinate various projects with multiple stakeholders.
  • A critical and constructive spirit, coupled with a capacity to challenge existing and contribute to the continuous improvement of team processes.

Why join us?

At Panerai, you will evolve in a dynamic and international environment, guided by a common passion for innovation and precision. We value the diversity of profiles, perspectives and experiences, putting each one's strengths at the service of collective success. We offer opportunities for continuous learning and development, so that everyone can build a successful career in Panerai.

Your recruitment path

After selecting your application, the process will begin with a first interview by Teams with our Human Resources team and the position manager. A second stage will take place on site, with the presentation of a business case and exchanges with your future peers.

Your next adventure starts at Panerai. We look forward to hearing from you.
