Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad in La Chaux-de-Fonds as a Builder, Builder Tools and be part of this extraordinary mission! We are a team of 5 employees and are currently looking for our next, next colleague to complete our #Squad! You will be attached to the site of La Chaux-de-Fonds . It's more than just a Builder's position, it's a position in which you can put all your knowledge to participate actively in the improvement and development of the activity. Your main mission will be to design, make and commission the tools and packaging necessary for the production of movements and watches. You will be responsible for your tools, from the expression of need to their validation in production: definition of need, design, choice of manufacturers, monitoring of costs, quality and deadlines, validation and serial monitoring. You will ensure that Breitling construction standards, as well as internal, NIHS and VSM standards, are met and that technical data (Vault, SAP) is updated.

Your contribution: Design and construction

  • You analyze applications for tools, define requirements with applicants and establish specifications for their validation.
  • You design production and control tools (layings, machining assemblies, assembly tools, templates and controls) for all sectors: Drafts, T0, T1, T2, T3, Washing Laboratory and Control.
  • You design technical packagings for the transport and handling of components.
  • You apply the Breitling construction standard by focusing on standard elements, codification of tools (trigrams) and reuse of proven solutions.
  • You make the technical drawings necessary for manufacturing (functional rating, tolerances, materials and treatments), as well as prototypes and simple tools in additive manufacturing (FDM, SLA, SLS) or PMMA cutting. Tooling folder control:
  • You manage your records from the definition of need to the validation of production tools, in accordance with the process and milestones defined by the Methods Office.
  • You plan and monitor projects by ensuring that costs, quality and deadlines are met, and anticipate any deviations by proposing corrective actions.
  • You lead design reviews with workshops, Industrialization and Quality for new tools and their modifications.
  • You organize your workload according to service priorities and ensure effective emergency management. Suppliers and procurement:
  • You select manufacturers according to the supplier matrix, view and compare their offers.
  • You manage purchase requests, order tracking and relationship with suppliers.
  • You receive and control tools and then coordinate the handling of potential non-compliances with manufacturers. Continuous improvement:
  • You identify and implement improvements to optimize tools, adjustment times and ergonomics at workstations.
  • You help solve tooling problems and help capitalize solutions.
  • You ensure a technological watch in the fields of additive manufacturing, materials and standard elements. General:
  • You communicate in a clear, factual and respectful manner with workshops, support services and suppliers.
  • You follow the work instructions, schedules and stamping rules, and represent the Methodology department with examples among workshops and suppliers. You think there's even more to find out about this job? You're right! Apply today to learn more! You will integrate perfectly with our team if:
  • You have a degree of ES technician in mechanical engineering or microtechnology, or a degree of HES engineer in an equivalent field.
  • You justify from 3 to 5 years of experience in the design and construction of tools or means of production, ideally in the fields of watchmaking, microtechnology or automation.
  • You have experience in conducting technical projects and tracking suppliers.
  • You master 3D CAD, ideally Autodesk Inventor, as well as the Autodesk Vault PLM and SAP ERP tools.
  • You master functional rating, ISO GPS tolerance and are familiar with NIHS standards.
  • You know the processes of watchmaking and making tools.
  • You master office tools, including Excel.
  • You are able to pilot suppliers and monitor projects according to the criteria of cost, quality and time.
  • You have knowledge of automation and operation of mechanical watch movements (advantage).
  • You know the problem solving tools (QRQC, 8D) and the methods of Lean Manufacturing (advantage).
  • You master French, both oral and written. Knowledge of English is an asset. We invite you to apply even if you do not meet all of these criteria.
