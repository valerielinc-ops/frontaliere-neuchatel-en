Maintenance Technician (Couvet Site)

Founded in 1921 in Mendrisio, Sintetica SA manufactures and conditions millions of doses of improved therapies in the field of anesthesia and analgesia. Our goal is to push the limits of science and technology by making therapies safer and easier to use. Thanks to close collaboration with clinical experts and ongoing engagement, we are improving treatment day after day.

In order to strengthen our team at the production site in Couvet, we are looking for a

Maintenance Technician

who will be responsible for ensuring the reliability, performance and conformity of our production equipment and infrastructure.

Your missions

Attached to the Maintenance & HSE service, you play a key role in the continuity and security of our operations:

  • Provide preventive and corrective maintenance of production equipment, including fillers, packaging equipment (packaging) and technical facilities at the site;
  • Monitoring and monitoring of facilities Facilities and utilities: purified water (PW), water for injection (WFI), steam, HVAC systems, air treatment and distribution, and monitoring systems. Participate in the commissioning and optimization of new lines;
  • Assist and train production teams in first-level maintenance;
  • Coordinate the interventions of external providers;
  • Manage spare parts and ensure the organization of the store (5S);
  • Document your interventions and participate in drafting procedures;
  • Actively contribute to continuous improvement, quality, safety and environment.

Qualifications required

  • Recognized technical training in industrial, electromechanical, automation or equivalent maintenance;
  • Minimum 5 year experience in aseptic pharmaceutical environment;
  • Ability to analyse, discipline, autonomy and team spirit;
  • Strong sense of safety, quality and continuous improvement.

We offer you

Permanent full-time employment in a stimulating and dynamic work environment where a positive and diverse culture prevails.

Sintetica promotes gender equality and encourages the personal evolution of its collaborators.

Join Sintetica and contribute to industrial excellence in a demanding and innovative environment.
