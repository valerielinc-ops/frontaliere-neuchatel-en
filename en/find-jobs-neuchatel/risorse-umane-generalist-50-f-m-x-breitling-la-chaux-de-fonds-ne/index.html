We are a dedicated Squad on a mission to redefine Swiss watchmaking, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision we create timepieces that combine modern-retro design with casual, approachable, and sustainable luxury — powered by passionate teams around the world. Join our vibrant team in La Chaux-de-Fonds as an HR Generalist (50%) and be part of something extraordinary! We are currently looking for our next colleague to complete our squad! It’s more than an HR Generalist role, it’s a role in which you’ll have the opportunity to make a meaningful impact across the employee lifecycle, support our people, and contribute to an international and dynamic environment.

Your contribution

  • You manage the administrative aspects of the entire employee lifecycle (hire to retire), including contracts, changes, and exits.
  • You maintain and ensure the accuracy of employee data in the HRIS.
  • You oversee operational absence management, including follow-up on medical certificates and coordination with APG and insurance providers, in collaboration with the HRBP when required.
  • You manage family allowances, including applications, updates, and liaison with relevant authorities.
  • You ensure the timely and accurate transmission of payroll variables to the payroll team.
  • You lead onboarding sessions for new hires, including administrative orientation.
  • You act as the first point of contact for employees regarding HR administrative matters.
