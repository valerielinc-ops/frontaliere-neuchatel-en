The Tertianum Group currently employs around 6'423 staff distributed throughout the Swiss territory, including around 1'100 employees and about fifteen sites in the region concerned.

Tertianum Chantevent in Fresens is looking for

Healthcare Assistant CRS 80-100%

Located in a green setting in Fresens, Tertianum Chantevent offers a peaceful living environment with a breathtaking view of Lake Neuchâtel. Specializing in the care of the elderly for long-term stays, the facility has 35 medical beds.

Your missions

  • Provide care under the delegation of qualified nursing staff;
  • Participate in the maintenance of the immediate environment of the residents (personal effects, furniture, etc.);
  • Contribute to the distribution of meals and snacks;
  • Observe and report any changes in the residents' condition to the responsible staff, and record this information in the care file.

Your profile

  • Mandatory Red Cross Healthcare Assistant Certificate;
  • Proven experience in geriatrics or psychogeriatrics;
  • Mastery of spoken and written French;
  • Listening skills, patience, team spirit, and availability.

Conditions

  • Hours: from 8h12, variable working days depending on the activity rate.
