Missions As part of the improvement of the performance of our mechanical workshop, we are looking for an engineer trainee to pilot a structural project to build up the monitoring of needle tools and improve visibility on production planning. Today, tools are distributed between storage areas (Kardex) and production, without a unified management system. This organization results in stock gaps, duplication, loss of visibility and difficulties in ensuring the availability of tools at the right time. Integrated into the mechanical workshop, you will be at the heart of the operations and work closely with production teams, methods and scheduling to build a pragmatic, sustainable and field-based solution.
  • Responsibilities Conduct a field diagnostic of the flows and practices related to tools (stocks, use, displacement) Build a unique and reliable repository of tools by eliminating gaps and duplications Develop and deploy an operational monitoring tool (digital or simplified) allowing a real-time vision Ensure the link between the availability of tools and production planning Define and implement simple, robust and applicable management standards by teams WHAT WE RECOGNIZE Engineering training, with specialization Independent, rigorous and proactive Team spirit and appreciative collaboration with diverse interlocutors and support change Pragmatism and strong field appeal Ability to understand operational issues and propose simple, effective and applicable solutions At ease with data analysis and digital tools (Excel in particular) and know how to structure information in a clear and usable way YOUR EXPERIENCE WITH US We invite you to submit your complete application directly to our careers website.
