Full description

Mission

We are offering a challenging position as a full-time (100%) PhD Student at STI IGM LAMD at EPFL, Neuchatel.

You will be working on the TurboImpact project, funded by Horizon Europe – MSCA-DN, tackles challenges in designing, optimizing, selecting materials, and manufacturing small-scale turbomachinery for applications in heat pumps, fuel cells, organic Rankine cycles, or gas turbines.

This project develops robust system and component design methods, an integrated manufacturing platform, and multi-material joining techniques to efficiently produce small-scale turbomachinery.

The goal will be achieved by establishing a network of 14 Doctoral Candidates (DC) working in synergy on complementary topics and supported by highly qualified supervisors from European universities and research-intensive European companies

Main duties and responsibilities

As a PhD Student, you will be expected to

  • Have full responsibility for your own dissertation
  • Research in close collaboration with industry partners
  • Experiment design and execution
  • Analyze and interpret experimental results
  • Write scientific articles for publication in peer-reviewed journals
  • Present at international conferences
  • Participate in education and supervise student projects

More specifically, within this project, you will c onduct research into how material properties, surface finish, and geometric tolerances affect the aerodynamic performance of small-scale turbomachinery, with a focus on understanding how degrees of design freedom related to manufacturing and material properties influence overall performance outcomes. Building on these insights, identify ideal aeromechanical turbomachinery designs that exploit new materials and manufacturing processes, and validate these findings through experimental comparisons of conventional and ceramic turbocompressor components manufactured using the novel processes developed.

Required competences

  • Strong background in fluid dynamics fundamentals, turbomachinery aerodynamics, aeromechanical design principles, experience with CFD & FEA.
  • Materials science, particularly properties of ceramics and conventional metals/alloys, manufacturing processes, surface engineering and its effect on flow behavior, geometric dimensioning and tolerancing.
  • Experience with turbomachinery testing procedures, instrumentation and sensor selection, data acquisition, uncertainty analysis and measurement validation.

Special requirements

At the time of recruitment, candidates must not have resided or carried out their main activity (work, studies, etc.) in the hiring Country for more than 12 months during the 36 months immediately preceding recruitment. • Applicants must be doctoral candidates (i.e., not already hold a doctoral degree) and be eligible under the MSCA Doctoral Network rules at the time of recruitment. • Short stays such as holidays, compulsory national service, or time spent as part of a refugee procedure are not considered.

Applicants must have a two-year master's degre in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Materials Science, Computer Science, Data Science, Software Engineering, or a similar degree with an academic level equivalent to a two-year master's degree.
