To strengthen our Research & Development department, we are currently looking for:

CAD–PLM–FAO Reference (Creo & Windchill)

Your Mission

At the heart of the digital chain, you are the key facilitator between the Technical Bureau, industrialization and manufacturing Your goal: to make Creo and Windchill real performance accelerators, by accompanying users, structuring methods and reliability of data, from design to industrialization. You have a global vision of the digital chain, from the CAD model to the manufactured component, and you like to transform tool constraints into simple, robust and efficient solutions.

How are you going to make a difference? Technical Offices (Hablets and Movements) Structure, build and simplify design to ensure robust, coherent and directly exploitable Creo models throughout the industrial digital chain.

  • Develop, test and deploy development methodologies (SDC, DDD, DDM, SENS Methodology...)
  • Create, maintain and evolve Creo templates, standard templates and configuration files
  • Automation and standardization of practices (macros, libraries, BT standards)
  • Continuously improve existing to improve quality and efficiency

Industrialization Transform CAD into efficient industrial data, automating methods and improving the efficiency of field users.

  • Developing new features Creo user-oriented performance
  • Automate and standardize industrialization deliverables (plans of dressing/movement phases)
  • Develop specific utilities (e.g. stacking, filling, etc.)

FAO Standardize and automate machining to secure programs, reduce preparation times and ensure consistent quality.

  • Structure and standardize process sheets and tool sheets
  • Define and deploy machining standards (steel, titanium, bronze, etc.)
  • Develop standard tool kits (universal, movement, dressing)
  • Creating universal starting tools and FAO structures
  • Develop, maintain and evolve existing postprocessors
  • Create postprocessors for new machines

Transversal & Support Be the reference point for users, ensuring support, coherence of tools and collective performance around Creo and Windchill.

  • Lead the network of CAD/PLM key-users / FAO
  • Manage user accounts, profiles and access rights
  • Support and resolve Creo / Windchill incidents
  • Ensure the stability and smooth functioning of the system and release
  • Train and accompany new employees
  • Provide first level support for design, industrialization and manufacturing
  • Participate in manufacturing cross-cutting projects
  • Develop and manage peripheral tools (JIRA, ERP interfaces, etc.)

What are the keys to your success in our House?

  • Degree in engineering (mechanical or computer)
  • Strong experience in mechanical design and industrialization
  • Good understanding of methods, data flows and industrial environments Richemont
  • Comfortable with project management and transversal environments
  • Service sense, pedagogy and continuous improvement
  • Advanced CAD Mastery (Creo): use, configuration, standards
  • Excellent control of PDM / PLM environments (Windchill)
  • Solid FAO skills: ISO language, machine tools, post-processors
  • LES knowledge and system interfaces
  • Database skills and development (SQL, HTML, JavaScript, PHP, VB.Net, Webservices)

You will work on a multicultural team and develop an international experience. You will develop and advance your career through opportunities within Maison and the Richemont Group.
