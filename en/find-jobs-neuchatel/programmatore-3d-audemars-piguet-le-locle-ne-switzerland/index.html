Full description

Attached to the head of quality control and working at the heart of the Manufacturing department, your mission will be to carry out the programs of the three-dimensional control machines and to carry out the corresponding checks.

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Achieving, validating and modifying 3D measurement programmes for new and existing products
  • Perform aesthetic and dimensional controls of our movement components
  • Analyze results and non-compliances
  • Carry out administrative procedures and propose solutions in collaboration with relevant sectors
  • Ensuring compliance with and improvement of the self-monitoring procedure in the workshop
  • You benefit from training in the field of quality, justify an experience of at least 5 years in a similar position in the watch industry
  • You master aesthetic and dimensional controls as well as Zeiss and OGP 3D machines
  • You have good knowledge of MS Office, control software and GPAO. Ellistat's knowledge is a plus.
  • Of a rigorous and proactive nature, you regularly offer ideas for continuous improvement and appreciate teamwork.

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
