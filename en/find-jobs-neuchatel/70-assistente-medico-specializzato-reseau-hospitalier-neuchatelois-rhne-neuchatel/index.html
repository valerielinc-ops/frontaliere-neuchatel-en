Full description

Our offer

  • The opportunity to play a key role in the development of angiology and to contribute to a stimulating medical project.
  • A collaborative working environment, fostering interdisciplinary exchanges and initiatives.
  • A balance between professional and private life, thanks to regular schedules, without guards, without picket or activity on weekends.
  • The possibility of considering a job sharing, for a work organization adapted to your aspirations.
  • The possibility of pursuing an academic activity, depending on the organization of the service.
  • Attractive conditions of engagement, governed by the CLC of senior physicians of the Neuchâtel hospital network.

Your missions

  • Exercise a clinical activity specializing in angiology within a dynamic and committed multidisciplinary team.
  • Ensure quality diagnostic and therapeutic management of patients with vascular pathologies.
  • Actively contribute to the development of angiology and the creation of a real centre of expertise in this field.
  • Work closely with different medical disciplines to ensure comprehensive and coordinated patient care.
  • Participate in the supervision and training of the medical ward.
  • Involve in the organisation, operation and evolution of the service

Your profile

  • Diploma of Swiss doctor or equivalent
  • FMH title of specialist in angiology or equivalent
  • Diploma and title registered in the Register of Medical Professions (MedReg)
  • Language level in French B2, certified
  • Confirmed clinical experience in the management of vascular diseases
  • Experience as head of clinic or deputy head of clinic desired

Your skills

  • Commitment, availability and accountability
  • Flexibility and adaptability
  • Educational and supervisory skills
  • Meaning of communication and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Autonomy, decision-making and implementation capacity
  • Interest in the development and organisation of a specialised clinical activity
